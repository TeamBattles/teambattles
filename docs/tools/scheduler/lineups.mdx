Battles Scheduler builds lineups from the players, availability, and pairings already on your Board
or Grid.

## Set the rules first

Open **Settings** and check these before your first run:

- **Players per match** - the usual lineup size. Individual matches can override it.
- **Minimum gap** - stops one player being put in matches that overlap or start too close together.
- **Overlapping matches** - when a player loses an overlap, decides whether they stay listed as a
  substitute or come off that match entirely.
- **Fairness** - how you want game time shared:
  - **Same number of matches** gives everyone a similar number of starts.
  - **Same share of chances** compares each player against the matches they were actually free for.
    Use this when some players are around far less than others.

## Run it

<Steps>
	<Step title="Choose how to watch">
		**Animated** plays the decisions out so you can follow them. **Instant** just applies the
		result.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Select Simulate">
		The lineups are built and applied. There is no confirmation step.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Read the result">
		Open the decision list, **Fairness**, or **Notes** to see short-handed matches, overlaps, Must
		play marks it had to break, and duties it could not fill.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Deal with anything blocking it">
		**Blockers** lists inputs that contradict each other, with fixes. Sort them out and run it
		again.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## What it cares about, in order

1. Not leaving an available player with no games at all.
2. Not using players marked Maybe.
3. Filling as many seats as possible.
4. Making the split fair, by whichever measure you chose.
5. Honoring together and apart pairings.

Each one beats everything below it. That is why a seat can be left open when the only way to fill it
was a Maybe player.

## Change it or try again

- **Run again** produces a different valid set of lineups, and keeps what you already have if the
  new attempt is worse.
- **Reset** clears the lineups but keeps your players, availability, duties, and pairings.
- **Undo** reverses the whole run in one go, including players it took off overlapping matches.
- **Move players yourself** any time - the final call is yours.

Change an input after a run and the lineup on screen is marked **out of date**. You can still share
it as it is, but select **Re-run** when you want the lineups to reflect your latest changes.

## Two tabs, or a small screen

A schedule can only be edited in one browser tab at a time. Open it in a second tab and you get a
read-only view - select **Take over** there to load the latest version and move editing across.

Phones and small tablets also get the read-only view. You can look through the lineups and open
Fairness, Blockers, and the sharing options, but you need a laptop or desktop to change or rebuild
the schedule.
