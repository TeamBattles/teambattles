## Which ones need an account?

Most of them do not.

- **Battles Replay** and **Battles Record** are apps you install and run yourself.
- **Battles Scheduler** keeps your schedules in your browser.
- **Battles Strategy** and **Battles Brackets** keep your work on your device until you choose to
  save it.
- The [Simple Overlay](/tools/battles-overlay/simple) works without signing in.

Sign in when you want to save and share strategies or brackets, keep an overlay of your own, or
connect Battles Bot to your account.
