## Using tools without an account

Battles Replay and Battles Record are standalone apps. Battles Scheduler keeps schedules in your
browser, while Battles Strategy and Brackets can keep work on your device without signing in. Sign
in when you want to save and share strategies or brackets, save a custom overlay, or link Battles
Bot to your TeamBattles account. The [Simple Overlay](/tools/battles-overlay/simple) also works
without an account.
