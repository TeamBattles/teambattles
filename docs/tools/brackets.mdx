Battles Brackets at [teambattles.gg/brackets](https://teambattles.gg/brackets) lets you build a
bracket for anything at all - a real tournament, a scrim night, a community event, or an argument
about who would win. It runs the same way TeamBattles [tournaments](/tournaments) do, but it is not
tied to any tournament, game, or real team.

You can build and score a bracket without signing in. Saving it to your account and sharing a link
need an account.

## Start a bracket

Pick a [format](/tournaments/formats) - single elimination, double elimination, round robin, or
Swiss - and paste in your entrants, one name per line. You need at least two.

Each format has a maximum number of entrants, and a longer list is trimmed to fit:

| Format                             | Maximum entrants |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Single and double elimination      | 128              |
| Swiss                              | 64               |
| Round robin                        | 32               |

You can also set the series length - Bo1, Bo3, Bo5, or Bo7 - before you build. Choose one for the
whole bracket, then override individual rounds underneath if you want the final to run longer than
the opening rounds. You can change any of it later.

<Note>
	Round overrides are tied to the rounds you can see, so they reset if the shape of the bracket
	changes - switching format, or adding enough entrants to add a round. Swiss only lists the rounds
	that exist up front; later rounds are paired as results come in and use your default until you
	change them.
</Note>

A live preview shows the bracket your list would build. Drag one name onto another to swap their
positions before you build, including taking over a free pass - the automatic first-round advance a
top seed gets when the number of entrants is not a power of two. **Reset arrangement** puts
everything back in list order.

**Build** saves the bracket in your browser and opens it. Nothing goes to your account until you
choose to save it, so this works signed out.

## Where your brackets live

The start screen lists everything you have, newest first, one card per bracket. A bracket you built
here and later saved to your account is still one card, not two. Each card tells you where it lives:

- **On this device** - built or opened here and never saved to your account. It works signed out and
  does not count towards your plan's limit, but clearing this browser's data deletes it.
- **Synced** - saved to your account, and this browser matches it.
- **Unsynced edits** - you have changes here that have not reached your account yet. Opening it uses
  this browser's version; save to bring your account up to date. This usually clears itself within a
  minute for a bracket already on your account.
- **Not in your account** - this browser has a copy of a bracket that is no longer on your account.
  It may have been deleted, or it may belong to a different account, so check which account you are
  signed in to. Either way your copy here is untouched. **Keep on this device** turns it into a
  normal on-this-device bracket; **Remove from this device** deletes it.

A bracket saved to your account is private to you. Opening its link asks you to sign in, and it only
opens for the account that owns it.

The header shows how many account slots your plan gives you, with a small meter. **Save to account**
still works when you are full - it offers to keep the bracket on this device instead, move an
existing one back to your device to free a slot, or upgrade.

## Name your entrants

Every entrant starts as plain text. You can also:

- **Rename** one at any time. It changes the label only and never touches results already scored.
- **Borrow a real team's name and logo.** Choose a game in the **Team lookup** box above the list
  first, then search for the team. This is cosmetic - the team is not involved and is not notified.
- **Upload your own logo.** It has to be an image under 1 MB, and you are told straight away if it is
  not. Logos are shown square and cropped from the middle, the same as team profile pictures, so a
  wide image loses its edges. Logos stay in your browser until you save the bracket to your account.
- **Clear a logo** to go back to plain text.

## Score it

Select a match to enter the result, or record a walkover. Results flow through exactly as they would
in a real tournament: winners move on, and in double elimination losers drop into the losers bracket.
Swiss rounds pair themselves once the current round is finished.

You can also:

- **Change the series length** for any round.
- **Disqualify** an entrant, which hands their opponent a walkover.
- **Shuffle seeds** to redraw the field. Shuffling several times in a row counts as one step, so
  **Undo** takes you back to where you started rather than to the fourth shuffle. Doing anything else
  ends the run.
- **Undo** one action at a time, exactly.

<Note>
	Changing the shape of the bracket clears the results, because the matches themselves change.
	That means adding or removing entrants, or switching a format option such as the grand-final
	advantage, the number of Swiss rounds, or round-robin passes.
</Note>

## Save, share, and download

<Steps>
	<Step title="Download a copy">
		**Download** has three options. **Export image** saves a picture of the bracket (see
		[Save it as an image](#save-it-as-an-image)). **Export project** saves a `.tbbrac` file with the
		whole bracket and any logos still only in this browser, so it travels complete to another
		computer. **Export animation** is coming soon. All of this works signed out and nothing leaves
		your device.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Save it to your account">
		**Save** asks for a **name** (up to 120 characters), an optional **description** for anyone you
		share it with (up to 2,000 characters), and who can open it - **Private**, or
		**Anyone with the link**. Your plan sets how many brackets you can keep; see the table at the
		bottom of this page.

		Once a bracket is on your account, it keeps itself up to date roughly once a minute. It only
		ever updates a bracket you already saved - it never creates one, so nothing lands on your
		account and no slot is used until you press **Save** yourself. Renaming does not trigger this;
		the new name goes up with your next save.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Share the link">
		Every saved bracket has a link, and **Share link** copies it. Whether anyone else can open it
		depends on the visibility you chose: **Anyone with the link** gives them a read-only view, and
		**Private** shows them a "not found" page. Copying the link of a private bracket tells you so
		and offers a shortcut to the setting. If you have not saved the bracket yet, **Share link**
		opens the save dialog instead, because a bracket has to be on your account before it can have a
		link.
	</Step>
</Steps>

### Save it as an image

**Download > Export image** saves the bracket as you see it on screen. You choose:

- **How much of it.** Drag across the preview to pick part of the bracket, or select
  **Whole bracket**. The size of the image you will get is shown underneath.
- **The file type** - PNG, JPEG, or WebP. PNG can also have a **transparent background**; the other
  two always have a solid one.
- **The size**, from half-size up to triple.
- **A TeamBattles frame**, on by default. It wraps the bracket in the TeamBattles look - the
  honeycomb background, a colored bar along the top, the logo, and your bracket's name - so the image
  still says what it is once it has been posted somewhere and the filename is long gone. It follows
  your own **Appearance** settings, so a bracket saved on the light theme matches the site you are
  looking at. Turn it off for a bare bracket to drop into your own graphic. The frame paints a solid
  background, so it and the transparent option cannot both be on.

Very large images are refused rather than quietly shrunk: if what you picked is more than your
browser can draw, **Export** stays greyed out until you select a smaller area or a smaller size. The
frame counts towards that, since it makes the image bigger than the part you selected.

Saving an image works on any plan, signed in or not, and nothing is uploaded.

<Note>
	Entrant names in Cyrillic or Vietnamese may appear in a different typeface in the saved image than
	they do on screen.
</Note>

## Managing your brackets

Select a card to open that bracket, or use its actions menu. Which actions you see depends on where
the bracket lives, and each one says exactly what it affects:

- **Open** - opens it.
- **Save to account** - for a bracket on this device, when you are signed in. It opens the bracket
  and starts the save; press **Save** there to finish.
- **Rename** - changes the name only, never the bracket or its results. Available for account
  brackets and on-this-device brackets. A rename on a device bracket stays in this browser.
- **Delete from account** - permanently removes it from your account after a confirmation, **and
  removes this browser's copy**. It frees a slot and cannot be undone. Files you downloaded and
  copies on your other devices are not affected.
- **Remove from this device** - removes only this browser's copy. If it is also on your account, it
  stays there and you can open it again. If it is not, this is the only copy, so download it first if
  you want to keep it.
- **Move to device** - copies an account bracket and its logos to this computer and removes it from
  your account, freeing a slot. It stops opening on your other devices and its share link stops
  working. Saving it back later gives you a new link.

If you have the same bracket open in another tab, that tab keeps up: a rename updates its title, and
a delete or **Move to device** tells it the bracket has gone and offers to take you back to your
list. Another computer needs a reload.

**Import from file** opens a bracket you downloaded earlier and adds it to this browser. It takes
both kinds of file the tool makes - a `.tbbrac` project file, and the older bracket file. This works
signed out. If a file cannot be opened, you are told why.

What comes back depends on the file:

- **A `.tbbrac` project file** brings back the logos that were still only in your browser when you
  saved it. Logos already uploaded to your account are not in the file, so a bracket saved to your
  account and then downloaded comes back with plain names. Each logo is checked the same way as one
  you upload by hand, and any that fails is left out, with a note telling you how many.
- **An older bracket file** has no logos or team links at all, so entrants come back as plain names.

Neither file identifies you, and neither reconnects to your account - an imported bracket always
arrives as an on-this-device bracket.

## When something goes wrong

### The same bracket changed in two places

If this browser has changes that never reached your account **and** the account copy has changed too
- usually because you edited it on another computer - you are asked which one to keep rather than
having it decided for you. **Keep this device's edits** uploads what is in front of you and
overwrites the account copy. **Open the account copy** throws away this browser's changes. Whichever
you do not keep is gone, and the bracket stays closed until you answer.

Only one side having changed is not a conflict and is never raised.

### Saving to your account has stopped

A banner appears when your bracket has stopped reaching your account:

- **The bracket is too large.** There is a size limit for a saved bracket, and one that grows past it
  stops saving. The banner shows the size and the limit. **Undo** is the way back under it, and your
  work stays in this browser meanwhile.
- **Saving keeps failing.** After several failures it stops retrying every minute. Your next change
  starts it again.
- **Logos have not uploaded.** The bracket itself is up to date, but one or more logos are still only
  in this browser. They keep trying, and they will not show on your share link until they get
  through.

### This browser is out of space

If you see **This browser is out of storage**, your changes have stopped being saved here. Brackets
already on your account are fine and still open normally. Free up space in your browser, or download
the bracket so you do not lose it. **Remove from this device** on brackets already saved to your
account reclaims space without touching your account. See
[Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting) for more.

## How many you can save

| Plan     | Brackets saved to your account |
| -------- | ------------------------------ |
| Free     | 3                              |
| Basic    | 10                             |
| Standard | 25                             |
| Premium  | 100                            |

At your limit you have four ways forward: keep the new bracket on this device, use **Move to device**
on one you no longer need in your account, delete one from your account, or upgrade. See the
[Quick Reference](/reference) for all plan limits.
