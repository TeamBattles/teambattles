Battles Scheduler answers the question every team manager knows: who plays which match, when
everybody is free at different times?

Add your roster and your match times, mark who can make each one, and Battles Scheduler works out
lineups that share the game time fairly. Change anything you disagree with, then copy the names out
or download a lineup image to post.

<Note>
	Battles Scheduler is free, needs no account, and keeps your schedules in your browser.
</Note>

![The Battles Scheduler Board before simulation, showing a player pool, ten match-time containers, and lineup settings](../images/scheduler/scheduler-board.png)

## Quick start

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open Battles Scheduler">
		Go to [teambattles.gg/scheduler](https://teambattles.gg/scheduler). You need a laptop or
		desktop to edit a schedule - phones and small tablets get a read-only view.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose how to begin">
		Start from scratch, copy a schedule you already made, open a saved schedule file, or load the
		example schedule and play with it.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Add your match times and players">
		Create a slot for each match, add your roster, and mark who can play when. Do it on the visual
		**Board** or in the spreadsheet-style **Grid** - whichever you prefer.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Build the lineups">
		Set how many players start each match and how you want the time shared out, then select
		**Simulate**. Review the lineups, the notes, and the fairness summary.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Share it">
		Copy the names, download a lineup image, or save a schedule file as a backup.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## What it takes into account

- Whether each player can, might, must, or cannot play each match
- How many players each match needs
- Matches that overlap or start too close together
- Players you want kept together, or kept apart
- Who can host and who can call
- Whether you want everyone to get a similar number of games, or a similar share of the games they
  were actually free for

You always have the last word. Move players yourself, ask for a different set of lineups, clear the
lineups and start over, or undo the whole thing.
