---
title: "Widgets"
description: "What each Battles Overlay widget shows on stream"
---

Battles Overlay has seven widgets, plus a rank widget for each game you play. Turn on the ones you
want in the editor and arrange them however you like.

Every widget fills in from your public profile, so nothing here needs a password or a key. If one
appears empty on stream, check [Visibility and Privacy](/tools/battles-overlay/visibility).

## At a glance

| Widget             | Shows                            | Size on screen |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------- | -------------- |
| User Stats         | Your own record                  | 300 x 120      |
| Team Stats         | A team you are on                | 300 x 120      |
| Organization Stats | An organization you belong to    | 280 x 100      |
| Active Match       | Your live match and its score    | 400 x 150      |
| Leaderboard Rank   | Where you sit on the leaderboard | 200 x 80       |
| Top 3 Players      | The current top three            | 250 x 180      |
| Referral Code      | Your referral code and total     | 200 x 80       |
| Game Rank          | Your rank in a specific game     | 320 x 100      |

Sizes are approximate, and the **Size** slider in the editor scales all of them together. You only
need them if you add each widget to your stream as its own source rather than using the full
overlay link.

## User Stats

Your own competitive record.

- Wins and losses
- Win rate
- Current win or loss streak
- XP

## Team Stats

Stats for one of your teams. Pick which team in the editor.

- Team name, tag, and logo
- The game the team plays
- Wins and losses
- Win rate
- Current streak

The team's profile has to be public for this to fill in.

## Organization Stats

Stats for one of your organizations. Pick which one in the editor.

- Organization name and logo
- Number of members
- Wins and losses
- Win rate

The organization has to be public for this to fill in.

## Active Match

Your current match, laid out as two teams facing off. It appears when a match starts and disappears
when you have none, so you can leave it on permanently.

- Both teams' logos and names
- The live score
- Match type and series length, such as "Best of 3"
- The current map, where the game has one
- A status label: **UPCOMING**, **READY**, or **LIVE**, with a pulsing dot once play begins

<Note>
	An empty screen where this widget sits is normal - it means you have no match on. It comes back on
	its own when one is scheduled or starts.
</Note>

## Leaderboard Rank

Where you place on the global leaderboard.

- Your position, such as "#12"
- How many players are ranked
- Win rate
- XP

## Top 3 Players

The three players at the top of the global leaderboard, each with their placing, name, avatar, and
win rate. This one always works, whatever your own settings are.

## Referral Code

Your referral code and how many people have used it - handy if you want viewers to sign up with it.

<Note>
	You need a referral code before this shows anything. Get yours on the [Referrals](/referrals)
	page.
</Note>

## Game Rank

A rank badge for a specific game, found under **Game ranks** in the editor.

- The game's rank badge
- Your tier or division
- Your SR
- Progress towards the next rank, where the game has it

A game can have more than one ranked mode, such as Ranked Play and Warzone Ranked. Turn each on
separately and they appear as separate widgets.

<Note>
	For Black Ops 7, enter your SR in
	[Settings > Game Stats](https://teambattles.gg/settings/game-stats). For Valorant and League of
	Legends, link your Riot account in Settings > Connections, then sync your rank from Game Stats.
</Note>

Game rank widgets have two extras in the editor's Style step:

- **Animation** - effects such as the winstreak flame, which you can switch off
- **Orientation** - stand the widget upright instead of laying it out sideways
