The OBS Dock is a Battles Overlay panel that lives inside OBS Studio. Turn widgets on and off,
change colors, and add or remove the overlay from your scene without switching to a browser.

## Before you start

- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) version 28 or newer
- A [TeamBattles](https://teambattles.gg) account

## Add the dock

<Steps>
	<Step title="Add the panel to OBS">
		In OBS Studio, open **Docks > Custom Browser Docks**. Give it a name such as "Battles Overlay"
		and paste this address:

    	```text
    	https://teambattles.gg/overlay/dock
    	```

    	Select **Apply**, and the panel appears in your OBS layout.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Connect it to your account">
    	The panel shows a 6-character code. Open the link it displays (or go to
    	[teambattles.gg/overlay/dock/authorize](https://teambattles.gg/overlay/dock/authorize)) while
    	signed in to TeamBattles, and enter the code.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Pick an overlay">
    	Once connected, the panel lists the overlays you saved in the editor. Choose one to start
    	working on it.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Adjust it live">
    	Turn widgets on and off, change the accent color, switch looks, and adjust size or transparency.
    	Your stream updates as you go.
    </Step>

</Steps>

<Warning>
	Sign in to TeamBattles in your browser before entering the code. The dock connects to whichever
	account enters it.
</Warning>

Codes last 10 minutes. If yours runs out, the panel makes a new one on its own.

## The two tabs

### Simple

The same one-widget game rank as the [Simple Overlay](/tools/battles-overlay/simple): pick a game,
enter your details, get a link. No account needed for this tab.

### Full

Everything from your saved overlays:

- **Widgets** - turn each one on or off
- **Style** - accent color, transparency, theme, size, and look
- **Team and organization** - choose whose stats to show
- **Scene sources** - add and remove the overlay from your OBS scene automatically

## Let the dock manage your OBS scene

The dock can talk to OBS directly, so turning a widget on also puts it into your scene. Without this
connection, the dock still works as a control panel - you just add and remove sources in OBS
yourself.

### Turn it on

1. In OBS, open **Tools > WebSocket Server Settings**
2. Tick **Enable WebSocket Server**
3. Select **Show Connect Info** to see the port and password
4. In the dock, enter the port if it is not the usual `4455`, then select **Connect**
5. Enter the password if you are asked for one

### What it changes

| Task                | Without the connection            | With the connection      |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Turning a widget on | You add the source in OBS         | Added for you            |
| Turning it off      | You remove the source in OBS      | Hidden for you           |
| Changing the link   | You update every source by hand   | Updated for you          |

## Dock or browser editor?

| |  OBS Dock | Browser editor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where you work | Inside OBS | In a browser tab |
| Widgets | Toggle in the panel | Toggle in the editor |
| Adds sources for you | Yes, once connected | No |
| Style changes | Apply straight away | Save, then refresh in OBS |
| Works with other software | OBS Studio only | Any streaming software |

<Tip>
	The dock suits streamers who live in OBS. If you stream from something else, or you would rather
	drag widgets around a preview, use the [overlay editor](/tools/battles-overlay/editor).
</Tip>

## Disconnecting the dock

Connecting the dock creates an entry named **OBS Dock** in your
[Developer Settings](https://teambattles.gg/settings/developer). Removing it signs the dock out
straight away. Connecting again from OBS sets it back up.

The dock can only read your own matches - it cannot post on your behalf or see anyone else's private
data. The technical detail is in
[Overlay Integration](/developers/overlay-integration#obs-dock-device-authorization).
