---

The Simple Overlay shows one thing: your rank in a game. There is no sign-in and nothing to save.
Pick your game, fill in the form, copy the link.

Open it at [teambattles.gg/overlay/simple](https://teambattles.gg/overlay/simple).

## Quick start

<Steps>
	<Step title="Pick your game and mode">
		Choose Call of Duty: Black Ops 7, Valorant, or League of Legends, then the mode you want to
		show.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Fill in your details">
		Enter your in-game name and choose how the widget should look. Black Ops 7 asks for your SR;
		Valorant and League of Legends ask for your region instead.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Copy the link">
		The link updates as you type. Copy it and add it as a browser source in your streaming
		software.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Supported games

| Game                      | Where the rank comes from        | Modes                       |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 | You type it in                   | Ranked Play, Warzone Ranked |
| Valorant                  | Pulled from your Riot account    | Competitive                 |
| League of Legends         | Pulled from your Riot account    | Solo/Duo, Flex              |

## Appearance options

| Option      | Choices                   | Default    |
| ----------- | ------------------------- | ---------- |
| Layout      | Rounded, Angular, Minimal | Rounded    |
| Orientation | Horizontal, Vertical      | Horizontal |
| Theme       | Dark, Light               | Dark       |
| Animated    | On, Off                   | On         |
| Size        | 0.5x to 2.0x              | 1.0x       |

## Keeping it up to date

**Valorant and League of Legends** refresh on their own every five minutes. Set it up once and leave
it running.

**Black Ops 7** shows the SR you typed in and does not change on its own. When your rank moves, go
back to [the form](https://teambattles.gg/overlay/simple), enter your new SR, and paste the fresh
link over the old one in your streaming software.

## Simple Overlay or the full editor?

| Feature                | Simple Overlay | Full editor           |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | --------------------- |
| Account needed         | No             | Yes                   |
| Widgets on screen      | One            | Seven, plus game ranks|
| Custom colors          | No             | Yes                   |
| How often it refreshes | Every 5 minutes| Every 30 to 300 seconds |
| Watermark              | Always shown   | Removed on Basic and up |
| Drag-and-drop layout   | No             | Yes                   |
| Saved overlays         | None           | Up to 5               |

<Tip>
	Start with the Simple Overlay. Move to the [full editor](/tools/battles-overlay/editor) when you
	want more than one widget, your own colors, or the watermark gone.
</Tip>
