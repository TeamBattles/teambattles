title: "Simple Overlay"

---

The Simple Overlay shows one thing: your rank in a game, or for Deadlock your record or last match.
There is no sign-in and nothing to save. Pick your game, fill in the form, copy the link.

Open it at [teambattles.gg/overlay/simple](https://teambattles.gg/overlay/simple).

## Quick start

<Steps>
	<Step title="Pick your game and mode">
		Choose Call of Duty: Black Ops 7, Valorant, League of Legends, or Deadlock, then the mode you
		want to show.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Fill in your details">
		Enter your in-game name and choose how the widget should look. Black Ops 7 asks for your SR;
		Valorant and League of Legends ask for your region instead. Deadlock asks for your Steam ID.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Copy the link">
		The link updates as you type. Copy it and add it as a browser source in your streaming
		software.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Supported games

| Game                      | Where the rank comes from        | Modes                       |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 | You type it in                   | Ranked Play, Warzone Ranked |
| Valorant                  | Pulled from your Riot account    | Competitive                 |
| League of Legends         | Pulled from your Riot account    | Solo/Duo, Flex              |
| Deadlock                  | Pulled from your Steam ID        | Ranked, Session, Last Match |

## Deadlock

For Deadlock, the form asks for your Steam account instead of a name. Paste your Steam ID or a
profile link into the **Steam ID or profile link** box, or type at least three characters of your
Steam name into **Search your Steam name** and pick yourself from the list. Each result shows an
avatar, a name, and how many matches that player has had in the last 30 days. The search only lists
accounts with recent Deadlock matches, so if you do not appear, paste your Steam ID or profile link
instead.

The **Steam ID or profile link** box accepts:

- Your 17-digit Steam ID, such as `76561198012345678`
- Your Steam account number, the shorter form of the ID
- The `[U:1:12345678]` form
- A numeric profile link, such as `steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198012345678`

Custom profile links, such as `steamcommunity.com/id/yourname`, are not accepted. The form tells you
so when you paste one: use your numeric profile link or the name search instead.

Ranked is your rank card. Session shows your record for today and the season, and Last Match shows
your most recent match. See [Widgets](/tools/battles-overlay/widgets#deadlock) for what each line
means. If there is no Deadlock data for the ID in the link, the source shows a dimmed "No Deadlock
data for this Steam ID" notice; see
[Troubleshooting](/tools/battles-overlay/troubleshooting#a-deadlock-widget-says-no-deadlock-data-yet).

Deadlock widgets in the Simple Overlay always show the teambattles.gg watermark, because a Steam ID
is not tied to a TeamBattles account. The [full editor](/tools/battles-overlay/editor) removes it on
Basic and up.

## Appearance options

| Option      | Choices                   | Default    |
| ----------- | ------------------------- | ---------- |
| Layout      | Rounded, Angular, Minimal | Rounded    |
| Orientation | Horizontal, Vertical      | Horizontal |
| Theme       | Dark, Light               | Dark       |
| Animated    | On, Off                   | On         |
| Size        | 0.5x to 2.0x              | 1.0x       |

Orientation and Animated only apply to rank widgets. Deadlock Session and Last Match have a single
layout, so the form hides both options for them.

## Keeping it up to date

**Valorant and League of Legends** refresh on their own every five minutes. Set it up once and leave
it running.

**Deadlock** refreshes on its own every two minutes. Stats for a match you just finished can take a
few minutes to appear.

**Black Ops 7** shows the SR you typed in and does not change on its own. When your rank moves, go
back to [the form](https://teambattles.gg/overlay/simple), enter your new SR, and paste the fresh
link over the old one in your streaming software.

## Simple Overlay or the full editor?

| Feature                | Simple Overlay       | Full editor              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Account needed         | No                   | Yes                      |
| Widgets on screen      | One                  | Seven, plus game widgets |
| Custom colors          | No                   | Yes                      |
| How often it refreshes | Every 2 to 5 minutes | Every 30 to 300 seconds  |
| Watermark              | Always shown         | Removed on Basic and up  |
| Drag-and-drop layout   | No                   | Yes                      |
| Saved overlays         | None                 | Up to 5                  |

<Tip>
	Start with the Simple Overlay. Move to the [full editor](/tools/battles-overlay/editor) when you
	want more than one widget, your own colors, or the watermark gone.
</Tip>
