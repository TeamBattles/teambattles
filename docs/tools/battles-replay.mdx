A browser extension for downloading Twitch and Kick VODs, recording live streams, replaying chat, and downloading from 1000+ sites via Battles Record integration. Local processing in your browser or remote downloads through the Battles Record desktop app.

## Features

<Note>
	All features work on both **Twitch** and **Kick** streaming platforms unless otherwise noted.
	Battles Record integration enables downloads from 1000+ additional sites.
</Note>

### VOD Downloads

- **Download full VODs** - Save entire broadcasts in one click
- **Custom time ranges** - Clip specific segments with precise start/end times
- **Unpublished VOD discovery** - Find and download VODs that streamers haven't made public yet _(Twitch only)_
- **Multiple formats** - Export as MP4, MKV, TS, or MP3 (audio only)
- **Quality selection** - Choose from Source, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, or Audio Only
- **Download queue** - Queue multiple downloads and track progress
- **Per-download controls** - Pause, resume, cancel, retry a failed download, or prioritize one to jump the queue
- **Bulk actions** - Pause All, Resume All, Cancel All, and Clear All, covering local and Battles Record remote downloads together
- **Survives restarts** - Paused and queued downloads are restored after a browser or service worker restart, resuming from the last completed segment

### Live Stream Recording

- **Rolling buffer** - Continuously buffers the last 10 minutes to 5 hours of a live stream
- **Create clips** - Save specific moments from the buffer (up to 30 minutes)
- **Save full buffer** - Download the entire buffer when the stream ends
- **Recorded source** - Optionally play a live stream from Twitch's own in-progress recording of the broadcast, which may avoid ads on some channels. It runs behind the live broadcast, and falls back to the normal live stream when it cannot be played _(Twitch only)_
- **Auto quality with adaptive bitrate** - Streams default to Auto quality; HLS.js selects optimal quality based on bandwidth with dynamic indicator (e.g. "Auto (720p60)")
- **In-player quality selector** - Gear icon in player controls opens a popover with all available qualities plus Auto option
- **Low-memory buffer** - Buffer stores URL metadata instead of binary data, reducing RAM usage by ~1.8 GB at Source quality
- **Buffer memory tracking** - Monitor buffer memory usage with 1 GB warning threshold
- **Chat history backfill** - Live chat loads up to 100 recent messages when opened

### Chat Replay

- **Synchronized chat** - View historical chat messages alongside VOD playback
- **Floating overlay** - Draggable, resizable chat window that works in fullscreen
- **Customizable appearance** - Adjust colors, opacity, font size, and message limits
- **Standalone mode** - Use chat overlay with Twitch's native player

### Live Chat <span className="text-xs bg-primary/20 text-primary px-2 py-0.5 rounded ml-2">New in v2.0</span>

- **Send messages** - Chat in live streams directly from the overlay
- **Emote picker** - Browse channel, global, and subscriber emotes with search
- **@Mention autocomplete** - Type @ to autocomplete usernames from recent chat
- **Follow button** - Follow channels directly from follower-only chat notices
- **Room state indicators** - See slow mode, sub-only, follower-only, and emote-only restrictions

### Mini Player

- **Picture-in-Picture** - Continue watching in a floating window while browsing
- **Drag and resize** - Position the player anywhere on screen
- **Persists across pages** - Mini player stays open as you navigate Twitch
- **Audio visualizer** - Visual feedback when playing audio-only streams
- **Snap-to-edge** - Mini player snaps to the nearest screen edge when released

### Audio Visualizer

- **Six visualizer styles** - Frequency Bars, Smooth Wave, Mirrored Wave, Mountain, Circular, and Dot Matrix
- **Visualizer picker** - A button in the top-right of the player while in audio mode opens a grid of styles, with live preview on hover
- **Audio-only playback** - The visualizer replaces the video surface when playing an audio-only stream or quality

### Keyboard Shortcuts <span className="text-xs bg-primary/20 text-primary px-2 py-0.5 rounded ml-2">New in v2.3.0</span>

- **50+ shortcuts** - Player, navigation, and download actions across the modal, browse view, VOD player, live player, and mini player
- **Familiar player controls** - YouTube/Twitch-style bindings: `Space`/`K` play-pause, `J`/`L` seek, `M` mute, `F` fullscreen
- **Navigation** - Switch tabs with `1`/`2`/`3` and jump to search with `/`
- **Help overlay** - Press `?` to see every shortcut available in the current context
- **Fully rebindable** - Remap any action, add multiple bindings per action, or disable shortcuts entirely from Settings
- **Playback speed** - Adjust VOD playback rate from 0.25x to 2x with `Shift+,` / `Shift+.`; persists across sessions

## How to Use

### Downloading VODs

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open a channel">Navigate to any channel page or VOD on Twitch or Kick</Step>
	<Step title="Click the Battles button">Find the Battles button in the page header</Step>
	<Step title="Browse and select a VOD">
		Use the search or browse the channel's VOD library (unpublished stream discovery is Twitch-only)
	</Step>
	<Step title="Set your options">
		Choose quality, format, and optionally set a custom time range
	</Step>
	<Step title="Download">Click download and wait for processing to complete in your browser</Step>
</Steps>

### Recording Live Streams

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open a live channel">Navigate to a channel that's currently streaming</Step>
	<Step title="Open Battles Replay">The extension will detect the live stream automatically</Step>
	<Step title="Watch and buffer">
		The stream buffers continuously in the background (configurable duration)
	</Step>
	<Step title="Create clips or save buffer">
		Use the clip tool for specific moments, or save the entire buffer
	</Step>
</Steps>

### Downloading via Battles Record

<Steps>
	<Step title="Install Battles Record">
		Download and install the [Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) desktop app
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pair the extension">
		Open Battles Replay settings, find the Battles Record section, and enter the 6-digit OTP code shown in the desktop app
	</Step>
	<Step title="Browse or paste a URL">
		Use the website dropdown to select a platform (Twitch, Kick, or Other), or navigate to any supported site
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pick format">
		Choose resolution, codec, file size, or audio-only from the format picker
	</Step>
	<Step title="Download">
		The download is handled by Battles Record on your desktop. Track progress in the Downloads tab with the Remote filter
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Authorization

<Info>
	API keys are **not required** for core functionality. Adding them enables enhanced features like
	badge images and additional metadata.
</Info>

The Authorization section in Settings allows you to configure optional API keys:

### Twitch User Access Token

An OAuth token for accessing the Twitch Helix API. This enables:

- Full badge images in chat replay (instead of letter abbreviations)
- Additional user metadata

You can either connect with Twitch (recommended) or manually provide a token with the appropriate scopes.

### TeamBattles API Key

Uses your linked Twitch account for authenticated API requests to TeamBattles services.

<Tip>
	**Quick setup with presets**: In [Developer Settings](https://teambattles.gg/settings/developer),
	click **Create API Key** and select the **Battles Replay** preset. This automatically configures
	the correct permissions (Matches: Read, Twitch: Enabled) with one click.
</Tip>

To create an API key manually:

<Steps>
	<Step title="Go to Developer Settings">
		Navigate to [Settings → Developer](https://teambattles.gg/settings/developer)
	</Step>
	<Step title="Click Create API Key">Enter a label like "Battles Replay Extension"</Step>
	<Step title="Set Permissions">
		- **Matches → User Matches**: Read - **Connections → Twitch**: Enabled
	</Step>
	<Step title="Copy Your Key">Copy the generated key immediately - it won't be shown again</Step>
	<Step title="Paste in Extension">
		Open the extension settings and paste your API key in the Authorization section
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
	Your keys are stored in the extension's sandboxed storage and are never shared or transmitted
	externally.
</Note>

## Download Formats

Choose the output format based on your needs:

| Format  | Best For    | Notes                                                |
| ------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **MP4** | General use | Universal compatibility, works everywhere            |
| **MKV** | Archiving   | Better metadata support, same quality as MP4         |
| **TS**  | Large files | No processing needed, fastest download, plays in VLC |
| **MP3** | Audio only  | Extracts and encodes audio track                     |

<Tip>
	For downloads over 1.2 GB, **TS format** is recommended. It uses minimal memory and creates a
	single file regardless of size.
</Tip>

## Large Downloads

Downloads estimated over 1.2 GB trigger a special prompt with format options:

- **TS (recommended)** - Single file, binary concatenation, minimal memory usage (~50 MB peak)
- **MP4/MKV** - Split into multiple part files (configurable chunk size: 500 MB - 1.7 GB)

Downloads over 30 GB are blocked to prevent browser memory issues.

### Configuring Chunk Size

In Settings, you can adjust the chunk size for large MP4/MKV downloads:

- **Range**: 500 MB - 1700 MB
- **Default**: 1000 MB (1 GB)
- **Warning**: Values above 1200 MB may cause memory issues

## Chat Replay

Chat replay displays historical chat messages synchronized with VOD playback.

### Features

- **Draggable overlay** - Position anywhere on screen
- **Resizable** - Adjust width (280-600px) and height (200-800px)
- **Auto-scroll** - Automatically scrolls to new messages
- **Fullscreen support** - Chat overlay works in fullscreen mode

### Customization Options

Access chat settings via the gear icon in the overlay header:

| Setting          | Description                        | Default |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------- |
| Timestamps       | Show VOD offset time per message   | Off     |
| Badges           | Display user badges                | On      |
| Fade when idle   | Hide UI elements when not hovering | Off     |
| Background color | Overlay background                 | #18181B |
| Text color       | Message text color                 | #FAFAFA |
| Opacity          | Overlay transparency               | 90%     |
| Font size        | Message text size                  | 14px    |
| Max messages     | Maximum messages displayed         | 200     |

### Limitations

- Chat replay is only available for **published VODs** (Twitch doesn't retain chat for deleted/unpublished streams)
- Sending messages only available for **live streams** (VOD chat is read-only)

## Changelog

<Accordion title="v2.6.0 - July 26, 2026">
### Added
- **Recorded Source for Live** - Twitch live streams can play from a recorded source (Twitch's own in-progress recording of the broadcast) that may avoid ads on some channels. A "Use recorded source" toggle in Settings controls it, on by default. The recorded source runs behind the live broadcast, so the player tag reads DELAYED and live chat runs ahead of the picture; turn the setting off to watch the channel's live stream instead

### Changed
- **Source Chosen When the Stream Opens** - The live source now follows your setting, either the recorded source or the normal live stream, instead of being resolved automatically. The player only changes source mid-stream if the one you chose turns out to be unplayable, in which case it falls back to the normal live stream
- **Steadier Live Buffer** - Latency and buffering targets scale with the stream's own segment length instead of a fixed number of seconds, and the forward buffer is deeper so one slow segment no longer drains it
- **Live Tag** - Reads DELAYED in amber with a static dot while a live stream plays from the recorded source, and LIVE in red otherwise; hover it for a short explanation. The tag appears in both the main player and the mini player, and the duplicate LIVE pill next to the stream title has been removed
- **Quality Menu Without HEVC** - On browsers without HEVC support, the recorded source's Source quality is left out of the live quality menu when other qualities are available, so playback picks one that decodes

### Removed
- **Live Ad Filtering** - Earlier versions tried to strip ad segments out of the live playlist, but Twitch inserts live ads into the stream itself and the filter could not reliably remove them. Live streams playing from the normal live source now show ads as Twitch delivers them; the recorded source, on by default, may avoid ads on some channels

### Fixed
- **Recordings Found on More Channels** - The recorded source and unpublished stream discovery now check every Twitch content server. Channels served by the ones that were being missed reported no recorded source and turned up no unpublished streams even when a recording existed; in a sample of 330 live channels, a little over half were on a server the extension was not checking
- **Unknown Content Servers Recovered** - When every known server comes up empty, the extension works out where the channel records to from its own most recent past broadcast and remembers it, so later streams and searches find it directly. This runs in the background and never delays playback
- **Live Autoplay and Resume** - Opening a live stream from a channel page starts playing instead of sitting paused, and reopening the extension on a loaded live stream picks it back up at the live edge. VODs still reopen paused where you left off
- **Channel Switching** - Switching to a different channel and reopening the extension discards the previous stream and loads the new channel's
- **Play Returns to the Live Edge** - Pressing play on a paused live stream jumps to the live edge instead of resuming minutes behind it; a deliberate rewind into the buffered part still keeps your position
- **Jump to Live in Audio-Only** - The "Jump to live" button now works in audio-only mode
- **No Catch-Up Speed-Up** - Live playback no longer speeds up to 1.1x to chase the stream, which shifted audio pitch and made motion look uneven
- **Recorded Source Stutter** - Live playback from the recorded source no longer freezes for a moment every few seconds
- **Large Segments and Source Lookup** - Large live segments no longer time out on slower connections, and looking up a live source can no longer hang
- **Black Screen at Startup** - The player keeps the loading spinner up until video is actually rendering, and falls back to the normal live stream if the recorded source cannot be decoded
</Accordion>
