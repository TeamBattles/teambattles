Battles Replay is a browser extension for Twitch and Kick. Save a past broadcast, record a live one
as it happens, and read the chat back in sync with the video. Connect it to the
[Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) desktop app and you can also save from YouTube, Instagram,
TikTok, and over a thousand other sites.

Everything happens on your own computer. Nothing you save is uploaded anywhere.

## What it does

<Note>Everything here works on both **Twitch** and **Kick** unless a feature says otherwise.</Note>

### Saving past broadcasts

- **Save a whole VOD** in one click
- **Save just a section** by setting a start and end time. Sections of 30 seconds or less are cut
  exactly; longer ones are cut without re-encoding, so they may start or end slightly off your markers
- **Find unlisted broadcasts** a streamer has not published yet, from the TeamBattles stream index,
  which has recorded streams since November 17, 2026 _(Twitch only)_
- **Choose a file type** - MP4, MKV, TS, or MP3 for audio on its own
- **Choose a quality** - Source, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, or audio only
- **Queue several at once** and watch them progress
- **Control each one** - pause, resume, cancel, retry, or push one to the front of the queue
- **Control all of them** - pause, resume, or cancel every download in the filter you are viewing,
  and clear finished ones, covering both your own downloads and any running through Battles Record
- **Pick up where it left off** after you close your browser or restart your computer

### Recording live streams

- **Keep recent history** of a live stream ready to save, from 1 to 120 minutes. It keeps links to
  the video rather than the video itself, so save while Twitch or Kick still serves it
- **Clip a moment** out of what has been kept, up to 30 minutes long
- **Save automatically** each time the kept history reaches its limit, if you turn it on
- **May avoid ads on some channels** by playing Twitch's own recording of the broadcast instead of
  the live feed. It runs a little behind, and drops back to the live feed if it will not play
  _(Twitch only)_
- **Quality that adjusts itself** to your connection, with the current choice shown in the player
- **Pick a quality yourself** from the gear icon in the player controls
- **Light on memory**, so a long recording does not slow your computer down
- **Chat from before you arrived** - up to 100 recent messages load when you open it

### Proxies

- **Bring your own proxy** - add up to 20 from the shield button beside Discord, or Settings >
  **Proxy**, as `host:port` or `user:password@host:port`. None are built in
- **May avoid ads on some channels** - Twitch decides whether a stream carries ads from the address
  that asks for it, so a stream requested through a proxy may come without them. It depends on the
  proxy and the channel, and it is not guaranteed
- **Two modes** - **Ad** sends only the requests that decide ads through the proxy. **All** sends
  every stream request through it. The video itself always downloads directly
- **Anonymous** - on by default, so live streams are requested as if you were logged out. Turn it
  off to use your Twitch login on twitch.tv, so subscriber and Turbo benefits apply
- **Never stops a stream** - if the proxy fails, the stream plays without it, and the shield button
  shows Inactive with the reason and a **Retry** button
- **Explained in the app** - the **?** button covers each setting and the downsides of using a
  proxy, such as slower starts and the proxy's owner seeing your address

<Note>
	Proxies apply to live Twitch streams in Battles Replay's own player. They do not change past
	broadcasts, Kick, or the player on twitch.tv. Chrome and Edge cannot sign in to a SOCKS proxy, so
	a SOCKS proxy there must be one without a username and password. If a VPN or another extension
	already sets your browser's proxy, Battles Replay leaves it alone and tells you.
</Note>

### Chat replay

- **Chat in sync with the video**, message by message, as it was sent
- **A floating window** you can drag, resize, and keep on screen in fullscreen
- **Your own look** - colors, transparency, text size, and how many messages to keep. It follows
  your theme until you pick your own colors
- **Works with Twitch's and Kick's own players**, not just ours. The chat button in their player
  opens it over the page, or in its own window if you set **Chat mode** to Window

### Live chat

- **Messages as they arrive** by default, or turn on **Follow video** in the chat settings to hold
  chat to the video when you pause or rewind
- **Send messages** without leaving the overlay
- **Emote picker** for channel, global, and subscriber emotes, with search
- **@ someone** and their name completes from recent chat
- **Follow** a channel straight from a followers-only chat notice
- **See the rules** - slow mode, subscriber-only, followers-only, and emote-only are all shown

### Mini player

- **Keep watching in a small floating window** while you browse
- **Or give it a window of its own** that stays on top of your other tabs and apps - set **Mini
  player mode** to Window. Turn on chat and it docks beside or below the video
- **See what is playing** - the channel badge and title sit in the corner and fade with the controls
- **Drag and resize** it anywhere on screen
- **It stays put** as you move around the site
- **Audio visualizer** when you are listening rather than watching

### Make it yours

Find these under Settings > **Appearance**. New users also see them in the welcome tour.

- **Theme** - Onyx, Navy, Graphite, Bone, or System to follow your device's light or dark setting
- **Accent** - Red, Amber, Cyan, or Lime for buttons, toggles, and progress
- **Corners** - Sharp, Soft, or Rounded
- **Density** - Comfortable, or Compact to fit more on screen
- **High contrast** and **Reduce motion** if you need them
- **View mode** - **Docked** opens Battles Replay over the page. **Floating** is a window you move
  and resize while you keep using the page. **Window** opens it in a separate window that stays on
  top when you switch tabs, windows, or apps
- **Chat mode** and **Mini player mode** - Floating over the page, or Window for their own
  always-on-top window
- **Floating trigger** - Swaps the Battles button beside the search field for one you can drag
  anywhere on the page

<Note>
	The Window choices need Chrome or Edge 116 or later, or Firefox 151 or later, on a computer.
	Browsers only open these windows when you click, so if Battles Replay opens some other way, a
	small card asks you to click.
</Note>

### Audio visualizer

Six styles - Frequency Bars, Smooth Wave, Mirrored Wave, Mountain, Circular, and Dot Matrix. When
you switch to audio only, the visualizer takes the place of the video. The button in the top-right
of the player opens the full set, with a preview as you hover each one.

### Keyboard shortcuts

- **Over 50 shortcuts** across the main window, the browse view, the players, and the mini player
- **The bindings you already know** - `Space` or `K` to play and pause, `J` and `L` to skip, `M` to
  mute, `F` for fullscreen
- **Get around** with `1`, `2`, `3` for tabs and `/` to jump to search
- **Press `?`** to see every shortcut available where you are
- **Change any of them** in Settings, add extras, or turn shortcuts off entirely
- **Playback speed** from 0.25x to 2x with `Shift+,` and `Shift+.`, remembered next time

### Working with Battles Record

- **Save from 1000+ sites** including YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok, handled by the
  [Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) desktop app
- **Connect by approval** - select **Connect** in the extension settings and allow it in Battles
  Record on the same computer, with no code to copy
- **Choose a format** - resolution, file size, or audio only
- **Unpublished Twitch streams too**, in Source quality, named after the stream and its date
- **Sign-in when you allow it** - turn on Twitch or Instagram account access so subscriber-only or
  private videos save; everything else downloads as a public visitor
- **See the connection** at a glance in the extension header
- **Send a channel to Battles Record** to be recorded automatically, straight from the player
- **Everything stays in step** across every browser connected to the same Battles Record
- **Errors in plain words** - when a download or library install fails in Battles Record, the
  extension says why

### Browsing beyond Twitch and Kick

- **Switch site** with the dropdown - Twitch, Kick, or Other
- **Play from anywhere** - open the extension from any page and video still plays
- **See where each download came from** at a glance
- **Filter your downloads** by All, your own, or ones running through Battles Record

### Following and Discover

- **Star a channel** from any channel card to add it to your list
- **Discover** shows your followed channels, who is live now, and their recent VODs in one place
- **Live channels stand out** with a red ring, a LIVE badge, the game they are playing, and a spot at
  the top of the grid
- **Follow from a search result** without opening the channel
- **Bring your follows across** from Twitch and Kick in one click from Settings
- **Get told when someone goes live**, if you want it - it is off by default

## How to use it

### Save a past broadcast

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open a channel">Go to any channel or past broadcast on Twitch or Kick</Step>
	<Step title="Select the Battles button">
		You will find it beside the search field in the page header, or wherever you dragged it if you
		turned on **Floating trigger**
	</Step>
	<Step title="Find the broadcast">
		Search, or browse the channel's past broadcasts. Finding unlisted ones is Twitch only. To look up
		a Kick video by its id, search `vod:channel/video-id`
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose your options">
		Pick a quality and file type, and set a time range if you only want part of it
	</Step>
	<Step title="Save it">Select download and let it finish</Step>
</Steps>

### Record a live stream

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open a live channel">Go to a channel that is streaming now</Step>
	<Step title="Open Battles Replay">It picks up the live stream on its own</Step>
	<Step title="Let it run">
		It keeps the last stretch of the stream ready in the background - you choose how long in
		Settings
	</Step>
	<Step title="Clip or save">Clip a specific moment, or save everything it has kept</Step>
</Steps>

### Save from other sites

<Steps>
	<Step title="Install Battles Record">
		Download the [Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) desktop app
	</Step>
	<Step title="Connect the two">
		With Battles Record open on the same computer, select **Open secure settings** in the
		extension's Settings tab, then **Connect** under Battles Record, and approve the request in the
		desktop app. Battles Record on another computer is not supported
	</Step>
	<Step title="Go to the video">
		Use the site dropdown to pick Twitch, Kick, or Other, or just open the extension on any
		supported page
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pick a format">Choose a resolution, a file size, or audio only</Step>
	<Step title="Save it">
		Battles Record handles it on your desktop. Track it in the Downloads tab.
	</Step>
</Steps>

	Want a long video as one file? Use [Battles Record](/tools/battles-record). The browser saves long
	videos in parts.

## Long videos

The browser works through a download in parts, so it never holds the whole video in memory at once.
When a download is estimated at over 64 MB, you are asked which format you want, and it is saved as
several files that play in VLC and most media players.

- **Part size** - each part takes up to 64 MB of video to begin with. Change it in Settings, anywhere
  from 16 to 128 MB. Larger parts use more memory
- **One file** - use [Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) for a long video as a single file

Anything over 30 GB is refused.

## Chat replay

Chat replay plays the chat back in time with the video.

- **Drag it** anywhere on screen
- **Resize it** to suit your layout
- **It scrolls itself** as new messages arrive
- **It stays visible** in fullscreen

Open the gear icon in the chat header to change how it looks:

- It only works for **published broadcasts**. Twitch does not keep chat for deleted or unlisted ones.
- You can only **send** messages in a live stream. Chat replay is read-only.

## Privacy

Video is processed on your own computer and saved straight to your device. From version 3.0.0, the
extension asks teambattles.gg for the history of channels you open in it and, while **Index streamers
I watch** is on, sends it the Twitch ids of channels you watch, follow or favorite. It never sends
your account or cookies.

Proxies you add stay in the extension on your computer and are used only to connect to that proxy.
Nothing about them is sent to TeamBattles. The proxy's owner can see your address and that you are
connecting to Twitch.

[Read the privacy policy](https://teambattles.gg/tools/battles-replay/privacy)

## Changelog

<Accordion title="v3.3.0 - November 24, 2026">
### Added

- **Proxies** - Add your own proxies and Battles Replay can send a live Twitch stream's requests through one. Twitch decides whether a stream carries ads from the address that asks for it, so this may avoid ads on some channels. It depends on the proxy and the channel, and it is not guaranteed. Open the new shield button beside Discord, or Settings > Proxy, add a proxy and turn on Use proxy. It covers live Twitch streams in Battles Replay's own player only, not past broadcasts, Kick or the player on twitch.tv
- **Saved Proxies** - Add up to 20 as `host:port`, `user:password@host:port`, or with `http://`, `https://`, `socks5://` or `socks4://` in front. None are built in. A proxy's password stays in the extension and the list shows only the address and username. Chrome and Edge cannot sign in to a SOCKS proxy, so a SOCKS proxy there must be one without a username and password
- **Mode** - **Ad**, the default, sends only the requests that decide ads through the proxy. **All** sends every stream request through it. The video itself always downloads directly, in both modes
- **Anonymous** - On by default, which is what earlier versions always did: live streams are requested as if you were logged out. Turn it off to use your Twitch login on twitch.tv, so subscriber and Turbo benefits apply. Your login goes only to Twitch, with that one request, and is never stored. With Anonymous off and the All mode, the login travels through the proxy: the proxy cannot read it, but Twitch sees your account coming from the proxy's address
- **Proxy Status** - The shield button's color and tooltip show whether the proxy is off, ready, in use, inactive or needs attention. A proxy that stops working shows Inactive with the reason (could not connect, too slow, wrong username or password, or refused by Twitch) and a Retry button. If ads still show through a proxy, the Proxy settings say so
- **Never Stops a Stream** - A proxied request that fails or takes longer than 4 seconds is sent again directly, so a bad proxy cannot stop playback
- **Proxy Help** - The ? button explains each setting and lists the downsides of using a proxy: no guarantee, slower starts, the proxy's owner sees your address, free proxies fail often, Twitch refuses some proxies, and Twitch sees your login coming from the proxy's location
- **Ad Tip** - The first time an ad shows on a live Twitch stream while Use proxy is off, a one-time tip points you to the Proxy settings

### Changed

- **Proxy Comes First** - With Use proxy on and the proxy working, a live Twitch stream plays the live feed through the proxy, whatever Use recorded source says. If that does not start, the earlier order applies
- **Changes Apply at Once** - Changing a proxy setting while a live Twitch stream is playing restarts the stream so the change takes effect
- **Connect to Battles Record by Approval** - No more pair code. Open Battles Record on this computer, select Connect in the Battles Record settings and approve the request in the app. If Battles Record's local service is stopped, the app offers to start it. If the app says no, or nobody answers within about two minutes, the reason is shown with a Try again button. Connecting a new browser needs Battles Record 2.0.0 or later; a browser already paired with an older version keeps working
- **Battles Record Errors in Plain Words** - Failed downloads and library installs from Battles Record say why in plain words, with Battles Record's own detail on a second line. Library installs no longer time out with an error while they are still running
- **Download Errors** - Failed downloads, and pauses or cancels that did not go through, show on a card that matches the rest of Battles Replay instead of plain red text
- **Narrow Window** - The Discord and Support buttons show just their icons whenever the Battles Replay window is 720 px wide or less, not only on a small screen
- **Removing a Channel from Recording** - Works in the player for a channel that was already in Battles Record's list

### Removed

- **Pair Codes and Remote Servers** - Battles Record on another computer is no longer supported. A saved remote address and its pairing are cleared on update. The Port field stays for a non-standard setup

### Fixed

- **Unpublished Streams in Battles Record** - Download via Battles Record works for unpublished Twitch streams instead of stopping at "Failed to extract video information". They download in Source quality and are named after the stream and its date. Needs Battles Record 2.0.0 or later
- **Battles Record Downloads List** - A download sent to Battles Record shows its title and a Twitch badge in Downloads straight away, instead of Untitled Stream and Other until you reloaded the page, and names its quality once, as you picked it
- **Battles Record Download Options** - Follow the video you switch to, instead of keeping the first video's title and formats

### Permissions

- **Chrome and Edge** - Ask you to approve a new permission after the update and keep Battles Replay turned off until you do. It is used only to send Twitch's stream requests through your proxy and to answer the proxy's own sign-in
- **Firefox** - Asks once, from the extension's settings page, the first time you need a proxy (Allow in settings)
- **Shared Routing in Chrome and Edge** - Chrome can only route by site, so while a stream is starting through the proxy, other Twitch requests from your browser may briefly use the proxy too, and other sites skip a proxy set in your computer's settings. With the All mode this lasts for as long as the stream plays. If a VPN or another extension already sets your browser's proxy, Battles Replay leaves it alone and says so
- **Nothing New Sent to TeamBattles** - The only new party is the proxy you add
