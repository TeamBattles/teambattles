Battles Replay is a browser extension for Twitch and Kick. Save a past broadcast, record a live one
as it happens, and read the chat back in sync with the video. Pair it with the
[Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) desktop app and you can also save from YouTube, Instagram,
TikTok, and over a thousand other sites.

Everything happens on your own computer. Nothing you save is uploaded anywhere.

## What it does

<Note>Everything here works on both **Twitch** and **Kick** unless a feature says otherwise.</Note>

### Saving past broadcasts

- **Save a whole VOD** in one click
- **Save just a section** by setting a start and end time
- **Find unlisted broadcasts** a streamer has not published yet _(Twitch only)_
- **Choose a file type** - MP4, MKV, TS, or MP3 for audio on its own
- **Choose a quality** - Source, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, or audio only
- **Queue several at once** and watch them progress
- **Control each one** - pause, resume, cancel, retry, or push one to the front of the queue
- **Control all of them** - Pause All, Resume All, Cancel All, and Clear All, covering both your own
  downloads and any running through Battles Record
- **Pick up where it left off** after you close your browser or restart your computer

### Recording live streams

- **Keep the last few minutes** of a live stream ready to save, anywhere from 10 minutes to 5 hours
- **Clip a moment** out of what has been kept, up to 30 minutes long
- **Save the lot** when the stream ends
- **Watch without ads on some channels** by playing Twitch's own recording of the broadcast instead of
  the live feed. It runs a little behind, and drops back to the live feed if it will not play
  _(Twitch only)_
- **Quality that adjusts itself** to your connection, with the current choice shown in the player
- **Pick a quality yourself** from the gear icon in the player controls
- **Light on memory**, so a long recording does not slow your computer down
- **Chat from before you arrived** - up to 100 recent messages load when you open it

### Chat replay

- **Chat in sync with the video**, message by message, as it was sent
- **A floating window** you can drag, resize, and keep on screen in fullscreen
- **Your own look** - colors, transparency, text size, and how many messages to keep
- **Works with Twitch's own player**, not just ours

### Live chat

- **Send messages** without leaving the overlay
- **Emote picker** for channel, global, and subscriber emotes, with search
- **@ someone** and their name completes from recent chat
- **Follow** a channel straight from a followers-only chat notice
- **See the rules** - slow mode, subscriber-only, followers-only, and emote-only are all shown

### Mini player

- **Keep watching in a small floating window** while you browse
- **Drag and resize** it anywhere on screen
- **It stays put** as you move around the site
- **Snaps to the edge** of the screen when you let go
- **Audio visualizer** when you are listening rather than watching

### Audio visualizer

Six styles - Frequency Bars, Smooth Wave, Mirrored Wave, Mountain, Circular, and Dot Matrix. When
you switch to audio only, the visualizer takes the place of the video. The button in the top-right
of the player opens the full set, with a preview as you hover each one.

### Keyboard shortcuts

- **Over 50 shortcuts** across the main window, the browse view, the players, and the mini player
- **The bindings you already know** - `Space` or `K` to play and pause, `J` and `L` to skip, `M` to
  mute, `F` for fullscreen
- **Get around** with `1`, `2`, `3` for tabs and `/` to jump to search
- **Press `?`** to see every shortcut available where you are
- **Change any of them** in Settings, add extras, or turn shortcuts off entirely
- **Playback speed** from 0.25x to 2x with `Shift+,` and `Shift+.`, remembered next time

### Working with Battles Record

- **Save from 1000+ sites** including YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok, handled by the
  [Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) desktop app
- **Pair once** with a 6-digit code
- **Choose a format** - resolution, file size, or audio only
- **Sign-in respected** so private or members-only videos save correctly
- **See the connection** at a glance in the extension header
- **Send a channel to Battles Record** to be recorded automatically, straight from the player
- **Everything stays in step** across every device connected to the same Battles Record
- **Connect to another computer** by entering its address

### Browsing beyond Twitch and Kick

- **Switch site** with the dropdown - Twitch, Kick, or Other
- **Play from anywhere** - open the extension from any page and video still plays
- **See where each download came from** at a glance
- **Filter your downloads** by All, your own, or ones running through Battles Record

### Following and Discover

- **Star a channel** from any channel card to add it to your list
- **Discover** shows your followed channels, who is live now, and their recent VODs in one place
- **Live channels stand out** with a red ring, a LIVE badge, the game they are playing, and a spot at
  the top of the grid
- **Follow from a search result** without opening the channel
- **Bring your follows across** from Twitch and Kick in one click from Settings
- **Get told when someone goes live**, if you want it - it is off by default

## How to use it

### Save a past broadcast

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open a channel">Go to any channel or past broadcast on Twitch or Kick</Step>
	<Step title="Select the Battles button">You will find it in the page header</Step>
	<Step title="Find the broadcast">
		Search, or browse the channel's past broadcasts. Finding unlisted ones is Twitch only.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose your options">
		Pick a quality and file type, and set a time range if you only want part of it
	</Step>
	<Step title="Save it">Select download and let it finish</Step>
</Steps>

### Record a live stream

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open a live channel">Go to a channel that is streaming now</Step>
	<Step title="Open Battles Replay">It picks up the live stream on its own</Step>
	<Step title="Let it run">
		It keeps the last stretch of the stream ready in the background - you choose how long in
		Settings
	</Step>
	<Step title="Clip or save">Clip a specific moment, or save everything it has kept</Step>
</Steps>

### Save from other sites

<Steps>
	<Step title="Install Battles Record">
		Download the [Battles Record](/tools/battles-record) desktop app
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pair the two">
		In Battles Replay settings, find the Battles Record section and enter the 6-digit code shown in
		the desktop app
	</Step>
	<Step title="Go to the video">
		Use the site dropdown to pick Twitch, Kick, or Other, or just open the extension on any
		supported page
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pick a format">Choose a resolution, a file size, or audio only</Step>
	<Step title="Save it">
		Battles Record handles it on your desktop. Track it in the Downloads tab.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Optional extras in Settings

<Info>None of this is required. Battles Replay works fully without it.</Info>

**Twitch badges.** Connecting your Twitch account (above) makes chat replay show real subscriber and
moderator badges instead of letter abbreviations.

**TeamBattles account.** You can link a TeamBattles key to bring your own match data into the
extension. Create one from
[Settings > Developer](https://teambattles.gg/settings/developer) with the **Battles Replay** preset,
which sets the right permissions in one click, then paste it into the extension's Authorization
section. Copy the key when it is shown - it is not shown again. Full details are in the
[developer documentation](/developers/authentication).

## Which file type to pick

| Type    | Best for    | Why                                                  |
| ------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **MP4** | Most things | Plays everywhere                                     |
| **MKV** | Keeping     | Holds more information about the video, same picture |
| **TS**  | Long videos | No processing, fastest to save, plays in VLC         |
| **MP3** | Audio only  | Just the sound                                       |

<Tip>
	For anything over about 1.2 GB, choose **TS**. It uses very little memory and gives you one file
	however long the video is.
</Tip>

## Long videos

When a download is going to be over about 1.2 GB, you are asked how you want it:

- **TS (recommended)** - one file, very little memory used
- **MP4 or MKV** - split into several parts. You can set how big each part is in Settings, anywhere
  from 500 MB to 1.7 GB, with 1 GB to begin with. Above 1.2 GB your browser may struggle.

Anything over 30 GB is refused, because no browser can hold it.

## Chat replay

Chat replay plays the chat back in time with the video.

- **Drag it** anywhere on screen
- **Resize it** to suit your layout
- **It scrolls itself** as new messages arrive
- **It stays visible** in fullscreen

Open the gear icon in the chat header to change how it looks:

| Setting          | What it does                                 | Default |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Timestamps       | Show how far into the video each message was | Off     |
| Badges           | Show subscriber and moderator badges         | On      |
| Fade when idle   | Hide the controls until you hover            | Off     |
| Background color | The chat window's background                 | #18181B |
| Text color       | The message text                             | #FAFAFA |
| Opacity          | How see-through the window is                | 90%     |
| Font size        | Message text size                            | 14px    |
| Max messages     | How many messages to keep on screen          | 200     |

### What chat replay cannot do

- It only works for **published broadcasts**. Twitch does not keep chat for deleted or unlisted ones.
- You can only **send** messages in a live stream. Chat replay is read-only.

<Accordion title="v2.6.0 - July 26, 2026">
### Added
- **Recorded Source for Live** - Twitch live streams can play from a recorded source (Twitch's own in-progress recording of the broadcast) that may avoid ads on some channels. A "Use recorded source" toggle in Settings controls it, on by default. The recorded source runs behind the live broadcast, so the player tag reads DELAYED and live chat runs ahead of the picture; turn the setting off to watch the channel's live stream instead

### Changed

- **Source Chosen When the Stream Opens** - The live source now follows your setting, either the recorded source or the normal live stream, instead of being resolved automatically. The player only changes source mid-stream if the one you chose turns out to be unplayable, in which case it falls back to the normal live stream
- **Steadier Live Buffer** - Latency and buffering targets scale with the stream's own segment length instead of a fixed number of seconds, and the forward buffer is deeper so one slow segment no longer drains it
- **Live Tag** - Reads DELAYED in amber with a static dot while a live stream plays from the recorded source, and LIVE in red otherwise; hover it for a short explanation. The tag appears in both the main player and the mini player, and the duplicate LIVE pill next to the stream title has been removed
- **Quality Menu Without HEVC** - On browsers without HEVC support, the recorded source's Source quality is left out of the live quality menu when other qualities are available, so playback picks one that decodes

### Removed

- **Live Ad Filtering** - Earlier versions tried to strip ad segments out of the live playlist, but Twitch inserts live ads into the stream itself and the filter could not reliably remove them. Live streams playing from the normal live source now show ads as Twitch delivers them; the recorded source, on by default, may avoid ads on some channels

### Fixed

- **Recordings Found on More Channels** - The recorded source and unpublished stream discovery now check every Twitch content server. Channels served by the ones that were being missed reported no recorded source and turned up no unpublished streams even when a recording existed; in a sample of 330 live channels, a little over half were on a server the extension was not checking
- **Unknown Content Servers Recovered** - When every known server comes up empty, the extension works out where the channel records to from its own most recent past broadcast and remembers it, so later streams and searches find it directly. This runs in the background and never delays playback
- **Live Autoplay and Resume** - Opening a live stream from a channel page starts playing instead of sitting paused, and reopening the extension on a loaded live stream picks it back up at the live edge. VODs still reopen paused where you left off
- **Channel Switching** - Switching to a different channel and reopening the extension discards the previous stream and loads the new channel's
- **Play Returns to the Live Edge** - Pressing play on a paused live stream jumps to the live edge instead of resuming minutes behind it; a deliberate rewind into the buffered part still keeps your position
- **Jump to Live in Audio-Only** - The "Jump to live" button now works in audio-only mode
- **No Catch-Up Speed-Up** - Live playback no longer speeds up to 1.1x to chase the stream, which shifted audio pitch and made motion look uneven
- **Recorded Source Stutter** - Live playback from the recorded source no longer freezes for a moment every few seconds
- **Large Segments and Source Lookup** - Large live segments no longer time out on slower connections, and looking up a live source can no longer hang
- **Black Screen at Startup** - The player keeps the loading spinner up until video is actually rendering, and falls back to the normal live stream if the recorded source cannot be decoded
  </Accordion>
