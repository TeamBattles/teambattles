## Account Issues

**"I can't log in"**

- Try a different login provider
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Check if the OAuth provider is having issues

**"I can't unlink my Discord/Twitch"**

- Keep at least one provider connected
- Link a different provider first, then unlink

**"My profile picture won't upload"**

- Check file is under 1 MB (banners are 2 MB). This limit is enforced on the stored file, so a browser that lets a larger file through will still be refused
- Ensure it's an image (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP). SVG is not accepted
- The image may have been flagged as inappropriate by our automatic content filter
- Try a different browser

**"That image could not be verified"** (`error_image_blob_unbound`)

- An avatar, banner or logo was named on a save that the server has no upload record for, or that belongs to a different profile, team, organization, league or tournament. The usual cause is a tab that has been open since before an update, so the page still holds an image reference the server no longer recognises. An image already in use on the item you are editing is always accepted, including one a co-admin uploaded.
- The same applies to a league, organization or tournament image supplied through the public API: branding writes only accept a storage id from `POST /uploads/image`, not one minted by `POST /uploads/image-url`.
- Fix: reload the page, pick the image again, and save.

## Organization Issues

**"I can't create a team"**

- You must be Owner or Admin
- Ask an admin to promote you or create the team

**"I can't invite members"**

- Managers can invite, Members cannot
- Ask an admin to promote you

## Team Issues

**"I can't join this team"**

- You must be in the parent organization first
- You may be banned from the team
- The team may be disabled

**"I was demoted when I promoted someone"**

- Each team has only one Captain
- Promoting someone to Captain makes you Co-Captain

## Match Issues

**"I can't start a match"**

- Only captains can start matches
- Both teams must have confirmed rosters
- Match must be "Accepted" status

**"I can't submit scores"**

- Only captains can submit
- Match must be "In Progress"
- Verify you have active players

**"I can't accept a match"**

- Your team must be for the same game
- You must be Captain or Co-Captain
- Meet platform/input requirements

## Chat Issues

**"I can't send messages"**

- You may be rate limited (5/minute)
- Wait for countdown timer
- Keep messages under 255 characters

**"I can't upload images"**

- Team may have reached 10 image limit
- Images must be under 2 MB
- The image may have been flagged as inappropriate by our automatic content filter

**"Where did my messages go?"**

- Chat is deleted 48 hours after match completion
- Screenshot important messages before match ends

**"I don't hear chat sounds"**

- Open Chat Settings and use both buttons under **Test tactical sounds**
- If the test reports that audio was blocked, allow sound for TeamBattles in your browser's site settings and test again
- Make sure **Enable notification sounds** is on; it is enabled by default only when this browser has no saved preference
- The room currently on screen stays quiet while the TeamBattles tab is visible and focused

**"I don't see browser notifications"**

- Browser notifications are opt-in even when tactical sounds are enabled
- Open Chat Settings, select **Enable browser notifications**, and grant the browser permission
- If permission was previously blocked, allow notifications for TeamBattles in your browser's site settings
- Browser notifications do not appear for the room currently visible in a focused TeamBattles tab

## Connection Issues

**"I can't link my Activision"**

- Ensure correct Activision account
- Log out and back in
- Clear browser cache

**"I can't play CoD without Activision"**

- Activision is required for Call of Duty
- Go to Settings > Connections to link

## Achievement Issues

**"My achievement didn't unlock"**

- Achievements unlock automatically
- Try refreshing the page
- Some need a short delay

**"The onboarding won't disappear"**

- Complete all tasks in each category
- Some tasks have specific requirements

## League Issues

**"I can't create a league"**

- League creation requires a TB staff-approved application. Navigate to Discover > Leagues > Create League to submit your application.
- Check if you have reached your league ownership limit (varies by subscription tier).

**"My team can't join a league"**

- Your team must have at least the minimum roster size required by the league.
- Check if your team is banned from the league.
- If you recently left the league, a cooldown period may apply.

**"I can't see my points or standings"**

- Points are only tracked during active seasons. Check if the league has an active season for your game.
- Scrimmage matches (outside active season) may not award points depending on league settings.

**"My league appears as orphaned"**

- This happens when the league owner's account is banned. Contact TB staff through the support system for assistance.

## Tournament Issues

**"I can't host a tournament" (`error_not_eligible_to_host`)**

- Hosting needs any paid subscription tier, or a Free account at least 30 days old.
- Competitively banned accounts cannot host.
- You may have reached your tier's limit on how many tournaments you can run at once (`error_tournament_host_limit_reached`) - finish or cancel an existing one, or upgrade your tier.

**"I can't set the participant count that high" (`error_tournament_size_limit_reached`)**

- The maximum participant count is capped by your subscription tier and by the format ceiling (128 for elimination, 64 for Swiss, 32 for round robin). Lower the maximum or upgrade your tier.

**"I can't add more sponsors" (`error_sponsor_tag_limit_reached`)**

- The number of sponsor tags is capped by your subscription tier. Remove a sponsor tag or upgrade your tier.

**"My team can't enter this tournament"**

- Registration may be closed or the tournament may be full (`error_registration_closed`, `error_tournament_full`).
- The tournament may use a different entry mode - an application- or invite-only tournament will not accept direct entry.
- Your team may already be entered (`error_already_entered`).
- Your selected roster must have at least the tournament's required team size (`error_roster_too_small`).

**"I can't join the free-agent pool"**

- The pool must be enabled by the host and open for joins.
- Some games require a linked platform account; link it in Settings > Connections first (`error_free_agent_account_required`).
- You cannot join a pool you are already in (`error_free_agent_already_in_pool`).

**"I can't open registration" (`error_approval_required`)**

- A tournament must be approved by staff before registration can open. If it is still awaiting review (`error_approval_pending`), wait for staff to approve it.

**"I can't check in" (`error_check_in_closed`)**

- Check-in is only possible during the check-in window, which opens a set number of minutes before the start.
- Your team must be approved into the tournament to check in.

**"I can't manage this tournament" (`error_not_tournament_staff`)**

- Only the host, host managers, and tournament staff can manage a tournament. Staff at the Moderator level cannot access the Staff or Settings tabs.

**"I can't save this bracket" (`error_sim_limit_reached`)**

- The number of brackets you can save to your account is capped by your subscription tier. At the limit, **Save to account** opens a panel with three ways forward: keep the bracket on this device, **Move to device** on a bracket you no longer need in your account, or upgrade your tier. **Delete from account** in a card's actions menu also frees a slot. You can still export a bracket to a local file for free.

## Battles Brackets Issues

**"A share link shows 'not found'"**

- A share link only works while the bracket's visibility is **Anyone with the link**. If the owner set
  it back to **Private**, or deleted it, the link shows a "not found" page. That is deliberate - a
  private bracket and one that never existed look identical, so nobody can go fishing for links.
- Ask the owner to change the visibility back. It is in the bracket's **Save** dialog, not on the
  toolbar - **Share link** only copies the link. The link is kept while a bracket is private, so the
  same one starts working again.
- If it is your own bracket, make sure you are signed in to the account that owns it. Your own link
  always opens for you, even while it is private.
- Check the link was copied whole. Chat apps and email programs sometimes cut long links short.
- Older-style links still work - they take you to the new address automatically.

**"Build does nothing and says 'Could not save the bracket'"**

- Battles Brackets puts each bracket in your browser before it opens, so it needs permission to store
  things. This message means the browser refused.
- The usual causes: a private browsing window, a setting or extension blocking site data for
  `teambattles.gg`, or a computer that has completely run out of disk space.
- Open the tool in a normal window, allow site data for `teambattles.gg`, or free up disk space. There
  is no way around it - a bracket has to be stored somewhere to be opened.

**"My bracket says 'Not in your account'"**

- This browser has a copy of a bracket that is no longer on your account. Either it was deleted, or
  it belongs to a different account.
- There is no way to tell those apart, so check which account you are signed in to first. Signing into
  the right one puts the card back to normal by itself.
- **Keep on this device** is safe - it keeps the bracket here and changes nothing about it.
- **Remove from this device** deletes the only copy you can still reach. Download it first if you
  want to keep it.

**"This browser is out of storage"**

- Your changes have stopped being saved on this computer. The editor still accepts them, but they are
  not being kept.
- Brackets already saved to your account are fine and still open normally anywhere.
- Download anything that only exists here, then free up space. **Remove from this device** on brackets
  already saved to your account reclaims space without touching your account.

**"My file would not open"**

- Files are checked before they are opened and the message says what went wrong - the file is not a
  bracket at all, it was made by a different version, it has fewer than two entrants, it has more
  entrants than its format allows (128 for elimination, 64 for Swiss, 32 for round robin), or the
  bracket inside it is damaged.
- A file an older version accepted can be refused now. That is deliberate: opening a bracket that
  fails a check would leave you with something that could never be saved.
- A project file keeps the logos that were still only in your browser when you saved it. Logos already
  uploaded to your account are not in the file, so a bracket downloaded after it was saved comes back
  with plain names. Every logo is checked the same way as one you upload by hand, and any that fails
  is left out with a note telling you how many.

**"That file is too large"**

- Refused on size before it is even opened. A project file holds every logo inside it, so a bracket
  with a lot of large logos makes a file too big to open.
- The message tells you the size and the limit. Nothing is wrong with the file, and nothing here is
  changed.
- Open the bracket where you made it, remove a few logos, and download it again.

**"That bracket is too large"**

- The file is fine. It is refused because the bracket inside it is bigger than one you could ever
  save to your account, so opening it would leave you stuck.
- Open the bracket where you made it, **Undo** a few times to shorten its history, and download it
  again. See "Saving to your account has paused" below for what makes a bracket grow.

**"An entrant image could not be verified"**

- One of your logos was not recognised as something this account uploaded. That happens when the
  bracket came from a file, from another account, or from a tab that has been open since before an
  update.
- Upload that entrant's logo again and save. Reloading the page first is the quickest way to be sure.

**"Some entrant logos could not be verified and were removed"**

- The bracket **did** save, just without those logos. When a save is refused over a logo, the editor
  drops the ones whose image is no longer on this computer, re-uploads the ones it still has, and
  tries again - this message means the second attempt worked. Your entrants, results, and seeding are
  untouched.
- It can appear without you pressing anything, since brackets save themselves in the background.
- Set those logos again and save. They upload normally from here.

**"Move to device failed"**

- Moving a bracket downloads its logos to this computer, and every one has to arrive. If any fails the
  move stops and your account bracket is left exactly as it was, so nothing is lost.
- Check your connection and try again. If it keeps failing, download the bracket so you have a copy
  either way.

**"Saving to your account has paused"**

- A banner appears when saving stops getting through, and it says which of three things happened.
  Your work is still being kept in this browser in all three cases.
- **The bracket is too large.** The banner names its size and the limit. Shuffling is what makes a
  bracket grow, because every shuffle is recorded so it can be undone, and each one stores the whole
  draw. A run of shuffles with nothing in between counts as one, so shuffling a fresh bracket is
  free - but **once the bracket has a result in it, every shuffle counts separately**, and a few
  hundred on a large bracket reach the limit. **Undo** back under it and saving resumes.
- **Saving keeps failing.** After several failed attempts it stops retrying every minute. Making any
  change starts it again. If it keeps failing, download the bracket so you have a copy that does not
  depend on your account.
- **Logos have not uploaded.** The bracket is up to date; one or more logos are still only in this
  browser and are still being retried. Until one gets through, those entrants appear without a logo
  to anyone opening your share link.

**"This bracket changed in two places"**

- This browser has changes that never reached your account, and the account copy has changed too -
  normally because the same bracket was edited on another computer. Rather than pick for you, the
  tool asks.
- **Keep this device's edits** uploads what is in front of you and overwrites the account copy.
  **Open the account copy** throws away this browser's changes. Whichever you do not keep is gone.
- The bracket stays closed until you answer, so nothing changes underneath you while you decide.
- If your answer cannot be saved because the browser is out of space, you are asked again and nothing
  has changed. Free up space and answer again.

**"This selection is too large to export"**

- Images are drawn in your browser, and browsers cap how big a single image can be. What you picked,
  at the size you picked, is over that cap, so **Export** is greyed out rather than handing you a
  blank file.
- Choose a smaller part of the bracket, or a smaller size. The readout under the preview updates as
  you go, so you can see when it fits.

**"The image could not be created"**

- The export started and failed part-way, usually because it was close to what your browser can draw
  or the tab ran out of memory. Nothing is downloaded and your selection is left as it was.
- Lower the size first - that helps most - then pick a smaller area. Closing other heavy tabs helps
  on a computer that is short on memory.

**"Saving a large bracket says 'Invalid input provided'"**

- Every save is checked against several limits, and all of them give this one message, so it does not
  say which. The ones you can reach while editing normally: the bracket's size, how many entrants its
  format allows (128 for elimination, 64 for Swiss, 32 for round robin), how long its result history
  is, and the length of its name.
- Size is the one you hit by accident, and the editor now explains that in a banner instead - see
  "Saving to your account has paused" above.
- **Undo** a few times to shorten the history, then save. Removing entrants is the other option.
  Downloading the bracket to a file always works, whatever its size.

## Tutorial Issues

**"My tutorial is stuck or not advancing"**

- Make sure you're clicking the highlighted element, not elsewhere on the page
- If the highlighted element isn't visible, try scrolling or navigating to the expected page
- Close the tutorial and restart it from the [dashboard](/dashboard)

**"The tutorial badge won't go away"**

- The floating "Tutorial in progress" badge means a tutorial is paused. Click it to cancel, or complete the expected action to resume.
- Paused tutorials are automatically abandoned after 24 hours.

**"I want to replay a tutorial"**

- Go to your dashboard and find the Tutorials section. Completed tutorials can be started again at any time.

## API Key Issues

Keys are only needed if you are connecting something to TeamBattles yourself. The
[developer documentation](/developers/authentication) covers them in full.

**"I can't create more keys"**

- You can have five at once. Delete one you no longer use to free up a slot.

**"My key isn't working"**

- Check you copied the whole thing. It starts with `tb_`.
- Check the key has the permissions the thing you are connecting needs.

**"I keep getting permission errors"**

- Edit the key and add the permission it is asking for.
- Permissions for a connected platform need that platform linked in
  Settings > Connections first.

**"I lost my key"**

- Keys are only shown once, when you create them. Make a new one and update whatever was using the
  old one.

## Battles Strategy Issues

**"I can't save any more strategies"**

- Your plan sets how many strategies you can save to your account. Delete ones you no longer need, or
  upgrade for a higher limit. See [Subscriptions](/subscriptions).
- You can always keep a strategy on this device instead - device strategies are unlimited.

**"I can't create any more boards"**

- There is a cap of 200 boards per account. Delete a board you no longer use before making a new one.
  Choose **Keep contents** when you delete it if you want the strategies inside to survive.

**"I can't upload any more custom maps"**

- Custom maps are a Premium feature, capped at 50 per person. Open the custom maps panel in the
  editor and delete ones you no longer use. You will need to move or delete any strategy still using
  a map before you can remove it.


## Link preview shows no image or a stale image

Discord, Twitter/X, and Slack cache link previews by page URL. If you shared a
link before a card existed, the platform may keep showing the old preview until
its cache expires. Pasting the link in a new message does not force a refresh.
If a preview is missing entirely, confirm the page is publicly reachable while
logged out - protected pages intentionally show the TeamBattles home card.
