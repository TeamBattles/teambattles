## Account Issues

**"I can't log in"**

- Try a different login provider
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Check if the OAuth provider is having issues

**"I can't unlink my Discord/Twitch"**

- Keep at least one provider connected
- Link a different provider first, then unlink

**"My profile picture won't upload"**

- Check file is under 1 MB (banners are 2 MB). This limit is enforced on the stored file, so a browser that lets a larger file through will still be refused
- Ensure it's an image (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP). SVG is not accepted
- The image may have been flagged as inappropriate by our automatic content filter
- Try a different browser

**"That image could not be verified"** (`error_image_blob_unbound`)

- An avatar, banner or logo was named on a save that the server has no upload record for, or that belongs to a different profile, team, organization, league or tournament. The usual cause is a tab that has been open since before an update, so the page still holds an image reference the server no longer recognises. An image already in use on the item you are editing is always accepted, including one a co-admin uploaded.
- The same applies to a league, organization or tournament image supplied through the public API: branding writes only accept a storage id from `POST /uploads/image`, not one minted by `POST /uploads/image-url`.
- Fix: reload the page, pick the image again, and save.

**"I can't create a team"**

- You must be Owner or Admin
- Ask an admin to promote you or create the team

**"I can't invite members"**

- Managers can invite, Members cannot
- Ask an admin to promote you

## Team Issues

**"I can't join this team"**

- You must be in the parent organization first
- You may be banned from the team
- The team may be disabled

**"I was demoted when I promoted someone"**

- Each team has only one Captain
- Promoting someone to Captain makes you Co-Captain

## Match Issues

**"I can't start a match"**

- Only captains can start matches
- Both teams must have confirmed rosters
- Match must be "Accepted" status

**"I can't submit scores"**

- Only captains can submit
- Match must be "In Progress"
- Verify you have active players

**"I can't accept a match"**

- Your team must be for the same game
- You must be Captain or Co-Captain
- Meet platform/input requirements

## Chat Issues

**"I can't send messages"**

- You may be rate limited (5/minute)
- Wait for countdown timer
- Keep messages under 255 characters

**"I can't upload images"**

- Team may have reached 10 image limit
- Images must be under 2 MB
- The image may have been flagged as inappropriate by our automatic content filter

**"Where did my messages go?"**

- Chat is deleted 48 hours after match completion
- Screenshot important messages before match ends

**"I don't hear chat sounds"**

- Open Chat Settings and use both buttons under **Test tactical sounds**
- If the test reports that audio was blocked, allow sound for TeamBattles in your browser's site settings and test again
- Make sure **Enable notification sounds** is on; it is enabled by default only when this browser has no saved preference
- The room currently on screen stays quiet while the TeamBattles tab is visible and focused

**"I don't see browser notifications"**

- Browser notifications are opt-in even when tactical sounds are enabled
- Open Chat Settings, select **Enable browser notifications**, and grant the browser permission
- If permission was previously blocked, allow notifications for TeamBattles in your browser's site settings
- Browser notifications do not appear for the room currently visible in a focused TeamBattles tab

## League Issues

**"I can't create a league"**

- League creation requires a TB staff-approved application. Navigate to Discover > Leagues > Create League to submit your application.
- Check if you have reached your league ownership limit (varies by subscription tier).

**"My team can't join a league"**

- Your team must have at least the minimum roster size required by the league.
- Check if your team is banned from the league.
- If you recently left the league, a cooldown period may apply.

**"I can't see my points or standings"**

- Points are only tracked during active seasons. Check if the league has an active season for your game.
- Scrimmage matches (outside active season) may not award points depending on league settings.

**"My league appears as orphaned"**

- This happens when the league owner's account is banned. Contact TB staff through the support system for assistance.

## Tournament Issues

**"I can't host a tournament" (`error_not_eligible_to_host`)**

- Hosting needs any paid subscription tier, or a Free account at least 30 days old.
- Competitively banned accounts cannot host.
- You may have reached your tier's limit on how many tournaments you can run at once (`error_tournament_host_limit_reached`) - finish or cancel an existing one, or upgrade your tier.

**"I can't set the participant count that high" (`error_tournament_size_limit_reached`)**

- The maximum participant count is capped by your subscription tier and by the format ceiling (128 for elimination, 64 for Swiss, 32 for round robin). Lower the maximum or upgrade your tier.

**"I can't add more sponsors" (`error_sponsor_tag_limit_reached`)**

- The number of sponsor tags is capped by your subscription tier. Remove a sponsor tag or upgrade your tier.

**"My team can't enter this tournament"**

- Registration may be closed or the tournament may be full (`error_registration_closed`, `error_tournament_full`).
- The tournament may use a different entry mode - an application- or invite-only tournament will not accept direct entry.
- Your team may already be entered (`error_already_entered`).
- Your selected roster must have at least the tournament's required team size (`error_roster_too_small`).

**"I can't join the free-agent pool"**

- The pool must be enabled by the host and open for joins.
- Some games require a linked platform account; link it in Settings > Connections first (`error_free_agent_account_required`).
- You cannot join a pool you are already in (`error_free_agent_already_in_pool`).

**"I can't open registration" (`error_approval_required`)**

- A tournament must be approved by staff before registration can open. If it is still awaiting review (`error_approval_pending`), wait for staff to approve it.

**"I can't check in" (`error_check_in_closed`)**

- Check-in is only possible during the check-in window, which opens a set number of minutes before the start.
- Your team must be approved into the tournament to check in.

**"I can't manage this tournament" (`error_not_tournament_staff`)**

- Only the host, host managers, and tournament staff can manage a tournament. Staff at the Moderator level cannot access the Staff or Settings tabs.

**"I can't save this bracket" (`error_sim_limit_reached`)**

- The number of brackets you can save to your account is capped by your subscription tier. At the limit, **Save to account** opens a panel with three ways forward: keep the bracket on this device, **Move to device** on a bracket you no longer need in your account, or upgrade your tier. **Delete from account** in a card's actions menu also frees a slot. You can still export a bracket to a local file for free.

## Brackets Tool Issues

**"A bracket share link shows 'not found'"**

- Share links live at `/brackets/view/<code>`. A link only resolves while the bracket's visibility is **Anyone with the link** - if the owner set it back to **Private**, or deleted the bracket, the link returns a 404 page. This is deliberate: a private bracket and one that never existed look identical, so no one can probe for codes.
- Ask the owner to set the visibility back. It lives in the bracket's **Save** dialog, not on the toolbar: the **Share link** button only copies the link. The code is kept while a bracket is private, so the same link starts working again.
- If you are the owner and still see 404, make sure you are signed in to the account that owns the bracket. Your own link opens for you even while the bracket is private.
- Check the code was copied whole. A share code is exactly 12 letters and digits, so a link cut short by a chat client or email wrapper resolves to nothing.
- Older links of the form `/brackets/<code>` are not broken - they redirect to `/brackets/view/<code>`. An older link whose code is not 12 characters sends you to the [Brackets tool](/tools/brackets) start screen instead.

**"Build does nothing and shows 'Could not save the bracket'"**

- The Brackets tool stores each bracket in your browser before opening the editor, so it needs site storage (IndexedDB). Build fails with this error when the browser refuses that write.
- Common causes: Firefox Private Browsing, a browser setting or extension that blocks site data for `teambattles.gg`, or a device that is completely out of disk space.
- Fix: open the tool in a normal (non-private) window, allow site data for `teambattles.gg`, or free up disk space. There is no in-memory fallback - the bracket has to be stored to be opened.
- The tool also asks the browser for persistent storage at this point. Some browsers decline that request; a declined request is not an error and is not the cause of this failure.

**"My bracket says 'Not in your account'"**

- This browser holds a copy of a bracket that is linked to an account bracket the tool can no longer find. Either it was deleted from the account, or it belongs to a different account.
- The tool cannot tell those two apart, so check which account you are signed into first - signing into the right one makes the card go back to normal on its own.
- **Keep on this device** is safe: it unlinks the bracket and keeps it as a device bracket, changing nothing about its contents.
- **Remove from this device** deletes this browser's copy, which is the only copy you can still reach. Export it first if you want to keep it.

**"This browser is out of storage"**

- Your bracket edits have stopped being saved on this device. The editor keeps accepting changes, but they are no longer being written to browser storage.
- Brackets already saved to your account are unaffected and still open normally on any device.
- Export anything that is device-only to a file right away, then free up browser storage. **Remove from this device** on brackets that are already synced reclaims space without touching your account.

**"My import was rejected"**

- Imports are validated at the door and the message says which check failed: the file is not JSON at all, it is not a bracket export, it is not a `.tbbrac` project file, the project file was made by a different version of the tool, the project file could not be read, it names a bracket format the tool does not recognise, it has fewer than 2 entrants, its entrants exceed the format's ceiling (128 for elimination, 64 for Swiss, 32 for round robin), its entrant ids are missing or duplicated, its seeding does not match its entrants, or its result history could not be read.
- A file that was accepted by an older build can be refused now. That is deliberate: a bracket that fails a check would otherwise be stored and then fail every save with a generic error.
- **A `.tbbrac` project file keeps the entrant logos that were still browser-only when it was exported**; logos already uploaded to your account are not in the file, so a bracket exported after it was saved to an account comes back with plain labels, the same as a plain `.json` bracket, which has its logos and team links removed outright. An imported logo still has to pass the same checks as one you upload by hand, and any that fails is dropped with a note saying how many were left out.

**"That file is too large" on import**

- Refused on the file's size before it is read at all. A `.tbbrac` project file stores every entrant logo inside it, so a bracket with many large logos can produce a file bigger than the tool can open.
- The message names the file's size and the limit. Nothing is wrong with the file itself and nothing on this device is changed.
- Fix: open the bracket where it was made, remove a few entrant logos, and export it again.

**"That bracket is too large" on import**

- The file is a valid bracket. It is refused because the bracket inside it is bigger than a bracket is allowed to be on your account, so importing it would leave you with something that could never be saved.
- The message names the file's size and the limit. Nothing is wrong with the file itself and nothing on this device is changed.
- Fix: open the bracket where it was made, **Undo** a few times to shorten its history, and export it again. See "Saving to your account has paused" below for what makes a bracket grow.

**"Saving my bracket says an entrant image could not be verified"** (`error_bracket_avatar_unbound`)

- An entrant's custom image was not recognised as one this account uploaded. That happens if the bracket came from a file, from another account, or from a tab that has been open since before an update.
- Fix: re-upload that entrant's image and save again. Reloading the page first is the quickest way to be sure the tab is current.

**"Some entrant logos could not be verified and were removed"**

- The bracket **was** saved, without those logos. When a save is refused over an entrant image, the editor drops the ones whose image is no longer on this device, re-uploads the ones it still holds, and tries once more; you see this message when that second attempt succeeded. Everything else about the bracket - entrants, results, seeding - is untouched.
- You can see this after a manual **Save** or after a background save, so it can appear without you pressing anything.
- Fix: set the affected entrants' images again and save. They upload normally from here.

**"Move to device failed"**

- Moving a bracket downloads its entrant images to this device, and every one has to succeed. If any download fails the move stops and your account bracket is left exactly as it was, so nothing is lost.
- Fix: check your connection and try again. If it keeps failing, export the bracket to a file so you have a copy either way.

**"Saving a large or heavily edited bracket says 'Invalid input provided'"** (`error_invalid_input`)

- Every save is checked against a set of stored limits, and all of them are refused with this one generic message - which limit was hit is not shown. The ones a normal editing session can reach: the bracket's stored size, its entrant count for the format (128 for elimination, 64 for Swiss, 32 for round robin), the length of its recorded result history, and its name length.
- Size is the one you reach by accident. See "Saving to your account has paused" below - the editor now explains a size refusal in a banner rather than leaving you with this message alone.
- Fix: **Undo** a few times to shorten the history, then save. Removing entrants is the other lever. Downloading the bracket to a file always works whatever its size.

**"Saving to your account has paused"**

- A banner appears in the editor when the background save stops getting through, and it says which of three things happened. Your edits are still being written to this browser in every case.
- **The bracket is too large.** The banner names the bracket's size and the limit. Shuffling is what makes a bracket grow: every shuffle is recorded so it can be undone, and each recorded shuffle stores the whole seeding, which is far larger than a reported result. A run of shuffles with nothing in between counts as one entry, so shuffling on a fresh bracket is free - but **once the bracket has a result in it, every shuffle is recorded separately**, and a few hundred of them on a 128-entrant bracket reach the limit. Fix: **Undo** back under the limit and the bracket starts syncing again.
- **Saving keeps failing.** After a run of failed attempts the editor stops retrying every minute. Making any change starts it trying again. If it keeps failing, download the bracket so you have a copy that does not depend on the account.
- **Logos have not uploaded.** The bracket itself is up to date on your account; one or more entrant logos are still only in this browser and are still being retried. Until one succeeds, those entrants show without a logo to anyone opening your share link.

**"This bracket changed in two places"**

- This browser has edits that never reached your account, and the account copy has changed since - normally because the same bracket was edited on another device. Rather than pick a winner, the tool asks.
- **Keep this device's edits** uploads what is in front of you and overwrites the account copy. **Open the account copy** discards this browser's unsaved edits and loads the account's version. The copy you do not keep cannot be recovered.
- The bracket does not open until you answer, so nothing is written under you while you decide.
- If the answer itself cannot be recorded - the browser is out of storage, or the write failed - the question is asked again and nothing has changed. Free up browser storage and answer again.

**"This selection is too large to export" (image export)**

- Images are drawn in your browser, and browsers cap how large a single image can be. The selected region multiplied by the scale is past that cap, so **Export** is disabled rather than handing you a blank file.
- Fix: select a smaller part of the bracket, or lower the scale. The size readout under the preview updates as you do, so you can see when it fits.

**"The image could not be created"**

- The export was attempted and failed part-way through, usually because the selection was near the limit of what the browser can draw or the tab ran out of memory. Nothing is downloaded, and your selection is left in place so you can shrink it and try again.
- Fix: lower the scale first - it is the cheapest lever - then reduce the region. Closing other heavy tabs helps on a device that is short on memory.

### Invalid cursor error (`error_invalid_cursor`)

If you receive a `400 error_invalid_cursor` response from `/api/v1/user/matches`,
`/api/v1/teams/[identifier]/matches`, or `/api/v1/orgs/[identifier]/matches`, the
cursor you passed was not produced by a recent response from the same endpoint.

**Common causes:**
- The cursor format changed between versions. If you stored a cursor from the
  legacy response envelope (`{ matches, pagination: { cursor, hasMore } }`),
  it will not work against the new native envelope (`{ page, isDone, continueCursor }`).
  Re-issue a fresh `numItems` request with no cursor to start a new pagination run.
- The cursor was truncated in storage or transit (e.g., URL length limits).
- A deploy invalidated server-side pagination state.

**Fix:** drop the stored cursor, call the endpoint again without `cursor`, and
use the `continueCursor` from the new response for subsequent pages.

## API errors

These error codes may be returned by the public REST API or by the Battles Strategy editor.

| Error code | HTTP status | Meaning |
| --- | :-: | --- |
| `error_invalid_num_items` | 400 | The `numItems` parameter was out of the valid range (typically 1-100 for paginated matches endpoints) |
| `error_invalid_limit` | 400 | The `limit` parameter was out of the valid range |
| `error_strategy_save_quota_exceeded` | 429 | The per-map or total strategy save cap for your subscription tier has been reached |
| `error_strategy_board_limit_exceeded` | 429 | The 200-board per-user anti-abuse cap has been reached |
| `error_custom_map_limit_reached` | 429 | The Premium custom-map per-user cap (50) has been reached |

**Fix `error_invalid_num_items` / `error_invalid_limit`:** clamp the pagination parameter to the documented range and retry.

**Fix `error_strategy_save_quota_exceeded`:** delete unused strategies, move strategies between boards, or upgrade your subscription tier for higher caps.

**Fix `error_strategy_board_limit_exceeded`:** delete unused boards from the Battles Strategy editor before creating new ones.

**Fix `error_custom_map_limit_reached`:** open the Battles Strategy editor's custom maps panel and delete unused custom maps. Custom maps are a Premium feature with a 50-per-user cap.

### League points table rejected (`error_invalid_input`)

`POST /tournaments/{identifier}/league-points` validates the placement curve before saving it. When the table itself is what failed, the 400 body carries a `reason` field alongside the usual `details` sentence, so you can branch on a stable code instead of parsing English. The table is validated even when `leaguePointsEnabled` is `false`.

| `reason` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `EMPTY` | The array has no rows. Omit `leaguePointsByPlacement` entirely to fall back to the platform default curve, or send `leaguePointsEnabled: false` to award nothing. An empty array is not the way to express either |
| `ALL_ZERO` | The table is well formed, but no row awards more than 0 points, so it could never score anything. Award at least one positive value, or disable league points |
| `TOO_MANY_ROWS` | More than 32 rows |
| `BAD_PLACEMENT` | A placement that is not an integer of 1 or greater |
| `BAD_POINTS` | A points value outside the 0-1000 range |
| `DUPLICATE` | The same placement appears twice |
| `NOT_ASCENDING` | Placements are not in ascending order |

`reason` is absent for the other 400 causes on this endpoint - a tournament that is not league-hosted, or one that has already finalized.

### 404 on a tournament that exists (`error_tournament_not_found`)

Three different situations produce this 404 on the Tournament API, and the response body cannot tell them apart. That collapse is deliberate: it stops the read endpoints from being used to enumerate the slug space.

1. **You passed an ID instead of a slug.** Every `{identifier}` in `/api/v1/tournaments/**` is resolved **by slug only** - the resolver does not accept a Convex tournament ID. Sending the `tournamentId` from the create response is the single most common cause. Store and send the `slug` instead.
2. **The tournament is not visible to you.** A `DRAFT`, or an `UNLISTED` tournament you have no relationship with, answers 404 rather than 403.
3. **There genuinely is no such slug.**

A sub-resource ID (`participantId`, `inviteId`, `offerId`, `rosterEntryId`, `nodeId`) that belongs to a *different* tournament also answers 404, for the same reason.

**Fix:** confirm you are sending a slug, then confirm the tournament is approved and visible to the key's owner.

### 403 on a tournament you do staff (`error_not_tournament_staff`)

The Tournament API checks two things independently: the capability on the key, and the key owner's **authority floor** on the tournament. Holding the capability is not enough, and a key can pass every scope check and still be refused here.

Seventeen organizer endpoints - update, submit for approval, registration open and close, round schedule, league points, cancel, start, regenerate matches, organizer roster add, roster entry delete, override result, revert result, add and remove staff, create and revoke invite - require the **ADMIN** floor. A tournament **`MODERATOR` does not meet it** and receives this 403 even though a `MODERATOR` can perform those actions in the web console's participant and match views.

**Fix:** promote the key owner to tournament staff `ADMIN`, or restrict the integration to the nine MANAGER-floor endpoints (applications list/approve/deny, participant remove and disqualify, staff list, invite list, activity feed, free-agent remove).

A TeamBattles staff member's key does **not** pass this floor by staff standing alone. Platform staff gain authority on a public API **write** only through explicit, audited elevation: the key must carry the `platform.staff` scope, the request must send `X-TB-Elevate: platform-staff`, and the key owner must hold the `management` developer role - all re-verified server-side. Organizer **reads** never elevate, and elevation never bypasses the owner-plan `tournamentOrganizerApi` feature gate. Without all three factors, or on any read, staff act through the web console (which offers a per-action "Act as Platform Staff" confirm). See [Authentication - Platform staff elevation](/developers/authentication#platform-staff-elevation).

### 403 `error_api_feature_required` on a tournament you host

Tournament organizer access is gated at `api_pro` on the **tournament host owner's personal developer plan** - the organization's owner for an `org`-hosted tournament, the league's owner for a `league`-hosted one, not the key owner and not the organization's own subscription.

An organization holding a consumer subscription does not satisfy this. The developer plan ladder has no organization arm.

**Fix:** the host owner needs the developer plan, not you and not the org. Their plan also sets the rate-limit ceiling for every organizer request on that tournament.

### The tournament started but there are no matches

`POST /tournaments/{identifier}/start` answers **`202`**, not `200`, and returns **before any match exists**. Rounds and bracket nodes are written synchronously; the matches themselves are generated by a background pass afterwards, which gives each node one attempt and then moves on. That pass can partially fail, and nothing in the 202 body reports it.

`nodeCount` in the response is the size of the bracket **structure**. It is not a match count and not a completion target.

**Fix:** poll `GET /tournaments/{identifier}/bracket` and look for nodes that have a `matchId`. If some never acquire one, call `POST /tournaments/{identifier}/regenerate-matches` (a separate `regenerate` capability, not `lifecycle`). Its `queued` value is an **upper bound** on what will be re-attempted, including nodes an earlier pass abandoned - `queued: 0` is a legitimate answer meaning nothing was eligible, not a failure. Regeneration is exactly-once per node, so it never destroys an existing match and is safe to repeat.

### An update returned 200 but the change is not live

`POST /tournaments/{identifier}/update` is **tri-state**. Branch on `applied` rather than on the status code:

| `applied` | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| `direct` | Never-approved tournament. The edit is live |
| `pending` | Previously approved. Material changes were **staged**, the tournament flipped to `AWAITING_APPROVAL`, and it has dropped off the public listing until staff re-approve. The edit is **not** live |
| `noop` | Previously approved, and every value sent already matched. Nothing was staged |

If an edit disappeared entirely, check for a misspelled key: fields the endpoint does not recognise are **silently dropped**, not rejected. A body with one valid key and three misspelled ones succeeds and applies only the one. Compare `changedFields` on the `pending` arm against what you meant to send. A body with *no* recognised fields is refused with `400` ("Supply at least one updatable field") rather than answering a misleading `noop`.

### Cannot remove a free agent (`error_invalid_input`, "Remove the player from the team roster first")

An organizer cannot withdraw a free agent whose status is `PICKED_UP`. The request is refused with `400` and **nothing is written**.

This is a guard, not a bug. Withdrawing them while their `FREE_AGENT` roster entry survives would strand the player: no re-entry path accepts a pool row that is not `AVAILABLE`, and the surviving roster entry keeps the re-pickup guards rejecting.

**Fix:** delete the roster entry first with `DELETE /tournaments/{identifier}/roster-entries/{rosterEntryId}`, which releases the agent back to `AVAILABLE`, then remove them from the pool. An `AVAILABLE` agent can be removed in one call. Note that `GET /tournaments/{identifier}/free-agents` lists `AVAILABLE` entries only, and the v1 reads do not project roster **entry** IDs to organizers - if you do not already hold that ID, remove the roster entry from the tournament management page in the web app.

### 429 during concurrent bursts from one key or IP (`error_api_rate_limited`)

If you fire many requests for the same API key (or from the same IP) at the same instant,
some may return `429 error_api_rate_limited` even when you are not obviously over your
sustained rate. Under a tight concurrent burst, the API sheds momentary write contention
on its rate-limit state by returning `429` rather than failing the request. Enforcement is
also shard-approximate at the margin, so the cutoff can land slightly before the exact
aggregate limit.

**Fix:** honor the `Retry-After` header (a conservative upper bound - a retry usually
succeeds sooner), add jittered backoff, and avoid firing a large batch of requests
simultaneously on one key. Treat `X-RateLimit-Remaining` as an estimate, not an exact
budget.

## Link preview shows no image or a stale image

Discord, Twitter/X, and Slack cache link previews by page URL. If you shared a
link before a card existed, the platform may keep showing the old preview until
its cache expires. Pasting the link in a new message does not force a refresh.
If a preview is missing entirely, confirm the page is publicly reachable while
logged out - protected pages intentionally show the TeamBattles home card.
