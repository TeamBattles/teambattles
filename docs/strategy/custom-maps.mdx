---
title: "Custom maps"
description: "Upload your own map images to plan on top of"
---

Custom maps let you bring any image into the strategy editor and use it as the background. Use them
for maps we do not support yet, scrim diagrams, whiteboard scenarios, or anything else you want to
draw plays on.

Each custom map has one level. Multi-level controls apply only to built-in maps that provide
registered levels.

<Note>
	**Premium only.** You need a Premium subscription to upload a custom map. Strategies already built
	on one keep working for everyone who can see them.
</Note>

## What you can upload

| Requirement   | Limit               |
| ------------- | ------------------- |
| File types    | PNG, JPEG, WebP     |
| Dimensions    | Up to 2048 x 2048   |
| File size     | Up to 10 MB         |
| Maps per user | 50                  |

## Big images

If your image is larger than 1440 pixels on its longest side, you are offered the chance to shrink
it before uploading. Smaller maps load faster on slower devices and take up less of your allowance.

Turn the offer down if you need every bit of detail.

## Checks before it appears

Every upload is checked for adult content before it turns up in your library or the map picker. This
usually takes a few seconds. Anything that fails the check is deleted and never appears.

## Markers

Once a map is uploaded you can pin up to 8 markers on it - spawn points, callouts, labels, whatever
makes the map easier to read. Markers are labels only: you cannot select or move them while you are
drawing.

## Use one in a strategy

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open the map picker">
		Either choose **Start a new strategy** on the welcome screen, or use **Change map** in the
		Layers panel of a strategy you already have open. Your uploads are under the
		**Custom Maps** tab.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pick your map">
		Choose one you uploaded. It becomes the background of the strategy.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Save">
		Save as usual. The strategy stays attached to that map.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Deleting a map

You cannot delete a map while any of your strategies still uses it. Point those strategies at a
different map first, or delete them. Once a map is gone, it is gone for good and the space is freed
straight away.

To replace a map, upload the new one, switch the affected strategies over to it, then delete the old
one.

<Tip>
	If you are close to your 50-map limit, clear out the ones you no longer use. Remember to move or
	delete any strategy that still points at a map before you can remove it.
</Tip>

## Sharing and exporting

Strategies on a custom map export exactly like any other - both **Export Frame** for an image and
**Export Animation** for video work normally.

<Warning>
	A custom map only appears for you. If you share a strategy built on one, or embed it, everyone
	else sees it without the background. Send them a downloaded **offline copy** instead, which
	carries the map image inside it.
</Warning>

If the map image cannot be found when you export - because it was deleted in the meantime - the
export still works. You get a plain rectangle where the map would have been, with your tokens,
paths, and drawings on top.
