---
title: "Collab Sessions"
description: "Plan a strategy live with up to 16 teammates at once"
---

A collab session turns the strategy editor into a shared whiteboard. Invite your team, draw
together, and point at things with the laser pointer while you talk it through.

<Note>
	**You need Premium to host.** Anyone you invite can join without a subscription.
</Note>

## Start a session

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open the strategy editor">
		Go to [teambattles.gg/strategy](https://teambattles.gg/strategy) and open or create a strategy.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Select Start collab session">
		It is in the editor toolbar. The session settings appear.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose a mode, a name, and a color">
		Pick **Collab** or **Presenter**, set the name your teammates will see, and pick a color.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Send out the link">
		The link is in the participants panel, hidden by default so it does not end up on camera.
		Reveal it when you are ready, then copy and send it.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
	If you have not saved the strategy yet, starting a session saves it to your account for you. There
	is no separate save step.
</Note>

## Collab or Presenter?

<Tabs>
	<Tab title="Collab">
		Everyone can draw, select shapes, and move tokens. Use this when the whole team is planning
		together.
	</Tab>
	<Tab title="Presenter">
		Only you drive the board. Built for walkthroughs and coaching. You can still let guests in
		partway:

		- **Guests can draw** - they can add shapes, but not touch yours.
		- **Guests can select** - they can select and move shapes you have placed.

		Turn either on or off at any point during the session from the participants panel.
	</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Levels in a session

On a built-in map with multiple levels, the selected level belongs to the current stage. Stage-level
changes and **Visible on levels** token choices sync with the rest of the board, so participants,
saved strategies, local drafts, and exported project files keep the same level behavior.

## Change mode partway through

You do not have to restart to change how your team takes part. Open the participants panel and use
the **Mode** control - only you see it.

- Switch between **Collab** and **Presenter** whenever you like.
- In **Presenter**, turn **Guests can draw** and **Guests can select** on and off independently.
  Guests get the change within about a second.
- Switching back to **Collab** gives everyone drawing and selection again, so those two switches
  disappear.

This is separate from **Lock board**, which is a quick freeze rather than a change of mode.

## Join a session

Anyone with the link can join, signed in or not. There is no invite list.

<Steps>
	<Step title="Open the link">
		The host sends you a link starting `https://teambattles.gg/strategy/session/`.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pick a name and color">
		Choose the name everyone else will see, and a color that is still free. If two people grab the
		same color at once, one of you is moved to the next free one automatically.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Get to work">
		You will see everyone's cursors moving live. If you are allowed to edit, what you place and
		draw shows up for everyone within about a second.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
	A session holds **16 people** at once - one per color. Once every color is taken, nobody else can
	join until somebody leaves.
</Note>

## Drawing together

In Collab mode, or in Presenter mode with drawing allowed, everyone shares one board. Whatever you
place, draw, or sketch is saved to the host's strategy and appears for everyone within about a
second.

A few things worth knowing:

- The **pen leaves a permanent line**, not a fading trail. Use the laser pointer for that.
- Someone else's changes show up straight away, apart from the exact shape you happen to be editing
  at that moment.
- Only the host can add, remove, or rename **stages**. Everyone else works on the stages that exist.
- In Presenter mode with drawing off, guests can only watch - but everyone can still use the laser
  pointer.

## Laser pointer

Pick the **Laser** tool and drag on the board to show a fading trail everyone can see. It leaves
nothing behind, so unlike the pen it is available to **everyone**, including people who are only
watching. Use it to point at a spot or trace a route while you talk. Hold **Space** and drag to move
around the board while the laser is selected.

## Freeze the board

As the host you can stop everyone else editing in one click: **Lock board** in the participants
panel. While it is locked, guests cannot place, draw, or move anything, their editing panels hide,
and they see a note explaining why. You can still work, and everybody can still use the laser
pointer. Unlock it to carry on together.

## Keep the link off camera

The session link is hidden behind a reveal toggle in the participants panel, so it does not show up
on your stream. Reveal it only when you are copying it, then hide it again.

## Remove someone

Find them in the participants panel and select the remove icon beside their name. They leave
straight away. They can rejoin with the link while the session is still running, so only do this if
you are also going to stop sharing the link, or end the session.

## End a session

- **End it yourself.** Select **End session** in the participants panel. Everyone is returned to the
  strategy view.
- **It ends on its own.** If you go quiet for five minutes, the session closes automatically.

<Tip>
	Everything done during a session is saved to the strategy as it happens, so it is all still there
	afterwards. There is nothing to save at the end.
</Tip>
