---
title: "Managing your boards"
description: "Group strategies into a board, file them by dragging, share a board, and choose what happens when you delete one"
---

A **board** is a folder for your strategies. Use one to keep related plays together - a map pool for
an upcoming tournament, scouting notes on a rival, or a scratchpad of ideas.

Each board has its own name, description, visibility, and shareable page. The strategies inside keep
their own visibility settings on top of the board's.

For working with individual strategies, see
[Managing your strategies](/strategy/managing-strategies).

## Create a board

Open **My boards** from the editor header and choose **New board**.

<Steps>
	<Step title="Give it a name">
		Shown in My Boards and on the board's shared page. Up to 120 characters.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Add a description (optional)">
		A short line that appears under the name on the shared page.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose who can see it">
		**Private** (only you), **Unlisted** (anyone with the link), or **Public** (anyone can find it).
		You can change this later.
	</Step>
</Steps>

A new board starts empty. Move existing strategies into it, or open the board and choose
**New strategy** to create one straight into it.

## Put strategies in a board

Strategies not in any board sit in **Ungrouped** at the top of My Boards. Two ways to file them:

<Steps>
	<Step title="Drag them">
		Drag a strategy card from **Ungrouped**, or from another board, onto a board in the list. Drag
		one back onto **Ungrouped** to take it out again.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Use the menu">
		Open the `...` menu on any saved strategy and choose **Move to board**.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
	Only you can rearrange your own boards. Someone visiting your shared board sees the strategies but
	cannot move or remove anything.
</Note>

### Boards only hold saved strategies

Filing works for strategies saved to your account. Strategies kept on this device - the ones with a
**Local** badge - stay in the **Continue working** grid on the welcome screen and are not shown
inside a board.

If you are inside a board and save a device strategy to your account, it goes into that board
automatically.

## Share a board

Every board has its own page. What visitors see depends on the board's visibility:

<Steps>
	<Step title="Private">
		Nobody but you can open it. Anyone else gets a "not found" page - they cannot even tell whether
		the board exists. Use this for work you are not ready to show.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Public">
		Anyone with the link can open it, and they see the board's **public** strategies. Unlisted and
		private ones stay hidden.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Unlisted">
		Anyone with the link can open it, and they see both the **unlisted** and **public** strategies.
		Private ones stay hidden.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
	You always see everything in your own board, whatever each strategy's visibility is. The hiding
	only applies to other people.
</Note>

To get the link, open the board's actions menu in My Boards and choose **Copy link**.

### Embedding a board

Boards cannot be embedded yet. To put a play on a web page, embed one of the strategies inside it -
see [Sharing and embedding](/strategy/embedding).

## Delete a board

When you delete a board you choose what happens to what is inside:

<Steps>
	<Step title="Keep contents">
		Removes the folder but keeps every strategy in it. They go back to **Ungrouped**, where you can
		file them somewhere else. Choose this if you are just reorganising.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Delete contents">
		Deletes the board and everything in it. Choose this if you want the whole lot gone.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
	**Delete contents cannot be undone.** Deleted strategies cannot be brought back. A large board
	takes a few seconds to finish clearing out, and you can go and do something else while it does.
</Warning>

If you delete a board while one of its strategies is open, you are returned to a fresh canvas once
it finishes.
