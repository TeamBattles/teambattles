---
title: "Sharing and embedding"
description: "Share a strategy with a link, or put it on a web page so it plays there"
---

Once a strategy is saved to your account, it gets a link. Anyone you send that link to sees your play
on a read-only page and can watch it run through its stages.

You can also drop that same play into a web page of your own, where it plays in place - useful for a
team site, a wiki, a blog post, or a coaching write-up.

## Get the link

Open the actions menu on a strategy card and choose **Copy link**. Who can open it depends on the
strategy's visibility:

| Visibility   | Who can open the link                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Private**  | Only you                                   |
| **Unlisted** | Anyone you send the link to                |
| **Public**   | Anyone, and it can be found by other people |

Change the visibility any time from the same menu. See
[Managing your strategies](/strategy/managing-strategies) for the rest of the actions.

<Note>
	A strategy kept on this device has no link. Save it to your account first.
</Note>

## Put it on a web page

<Steps>
	<Step title="Save it and make it shareable">
		Set the strategy's visibility to **Unlisted** or **Public** in the Save dialog. A private
		strategy will not show for anyone else.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Take the address">
		Your embed address is `https://teambattles.gg/strategy/embed/` followed by the same code that
		is at the end of your share link.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Paste it into your page">
		Add it to your page with a snippet like this one:

		```html
		<iframe
			src="https://teambattles.gg/strategy/embed/your-strategy-code"
			width="600"
			height="600"
			frameborder="0"
		></iframe>
		```
	</Step>
</Steps>

You can also set how it behaves by adding options to the end of the address - whether it starts
playing on its own, whether it loops, whether visitors get play and stop buttons, which stage it
starts on, and how fast it runs. The full list is on the
[Strategy Embeds](/developers/strategy-embeds) page.

If a built-in map has multiple levels, the selected stage decides which level the embed shows. The
embed changes levels as playback or stage selection changes.

<Note>
	A strategy built on a [custom map](/strategy/custom-maps) only shows that map for you, so an
	embedded copy appears without its background to everyone else. Share it as a downloaded offline
	copy instead.
</Note>

## Controlling it from your own page

If you are building the page yourself and want your own play button, or want the strategy to jump to
a particular stage when a visitor selects something, that is covered in
[Strategy Embeds](/developers/strategy-embeds).
