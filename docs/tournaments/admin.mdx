title: "Administration"

---

Every tournament has a host admin panel at `/tournaments/[slug]/admin`. Hosts, host managers, and tournament staff use it to review entries, seed the bracket, run matches, and manage settings. Separately, TeamBattles staff review new tournaments from a platform-wide console.

## Who Can Manage a Tournament

| Role                        | Access                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Host managers**           | Full admin access. The hosting user, an org OWNER or ADMIN (for org-hosted), or a league OWNER or ADMIN (for league-hosted). |
| **Tournament staff - Admin** | Full admin access, including Staff and Settings.                                           |
| **Tournament staff - Moderator** | Day-to-day management (participants, matches, free agents, activity), but not Staff, Settings, submitting for review, or cancelling. |
| **TeamBattles staff**       | Platform staff who review and approve tournaments and can act everywhere.                   |
| **Team captains**           | Not tournament managers. Captains, co-captains, and org owners act on their own team's entry, roster, and check-in. |

## Admin Panel Surfaces

The admin panel is organized into tabs.

### Dashboard

The landing surface shows the tournament's approval state and the lifecycle actions available now - **Submit for review**, **Open registration**, and **Start tournament**. Cancelling is not on the Dashboard; it lives in [Settings](#settings-and-re-approval). An **approval banner** explains the current state whenever the tournament is a draft that has not been submitted yet or that staff rejected, or whenever it is awaiting review or has material changes pending.

While the tournament is a draft that has not been submitted or that staff rejected, the banner carries a **Submit for review** action, available to tournament admins. A rejected draft shows the reviewer's own rejection reason in the banner, so you know what to fix before resubmitting. Submitting normally sends the tournament to the staff review queue; under [relaxed review mode](/tournaments/create#what-approval-means) an **Unlisted** tournament is approved immediately and skips the queue.

### Participants

Manage everyone entered:

- **Participants** - the full table of entered teams and their status.
- **Applications** - approve or deny pending applications (for application-mode tournaments).
- **Seeding** - set the seed order (manual, random, or registration order) before the bracket is generated.
- **Check-in** - the check-in board showing which approved teams have checked in.
- **Disqualification and removal** - remove a team, or disqualify one. A disqualification propagates through a live bracket, awarding walkovers to the affected opponents.

### Matches and Bracket

View the generated rounds, nodes, and bracket, and step in when needed:

- **Manual result** - override a node's result directly. This is allowed only while the node's linked match has not yet started; overriding cancels the linked match and replays the result through the bracket.
- **Contested scores** - resolve a match whose result is in dispute by setting the series score.

Generated tournament matches are experience-only and published, are born already accepted, and get their own match chat, so teams play them like any other match.

### Free Agents

Manage the [free-agent pool](/tournaments/free-agents) - review who is available and remove agents when needed.

### Staff

<Note>Admin-only. Moderator-level staff do not see this tab.</Note>

Add or remove tournament staff and set each staff member's role (Admin or Moderator).

### Settings and Re-approval

<Note>Admin-only. Moderator-level staff do not see this tab.</Note>

Edit branding, presentation, and tournament settings. Editing a **material** field after the tournament has been approved sends only those changed fields back to staff for re-approval; the tournament keeps running on its last-approved values until staff accept them. Material fields include:

- Name, description, and rules
- Game, playlist, and team size
- Format and format configuration
- Participant range (minimum and maximum)
- Registration mode, registration window, check-in settings, start and end times
- Prize pool, currency, breakdown, and description
- Banner, logo, sponsor tags, social links, and visibility

Non-material edits apply immediately without review.

This tab also holds the danger zone with **Cancel tournament**, available until the tournament has completed or has already been cancelled.

### Activity

A chronological activity log of everything that happened - submissions, approvals, registration changes, participant actions, seeding, results, and staff changes.

## Staff Approval Console

TeamBattles staff review tournaments from a platform-wide console at `/admin/tournaments`:

- **Review queue** - tournaments awaiting approval, newest first, each with the host and format at a glance.
- **Approve or reject** - approve a tournament (or its pending material changes) or reject it with a reason. Rejecting a re-approval keeps the tournament running on its last-approved values and discards the pending changes.
- **Change diff** - for a re-approval, a field-level view of what changed (old value to new value).
- **Approval mode** - a platform toggle for how much review is required: review **all tournaments**, or only **publicly listed** tournaments (letting unlisted tournaments self-approve).
- **Stats** - summary cards for the review workload.
