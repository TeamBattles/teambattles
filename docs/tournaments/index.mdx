Tournaments let you run bracketed competition on TeamBattles - single elimination, double elimination, round robin, and Swiss. The platform generates the matches, tracks results through the bracket automatically, and finalizes placements when the final is decided.

## What Tournaments Offer

- **Four formats** - Single elimination, double elimination, round robin, and Swiss, with per-round best-of control
- **Automated brackets** - Matches are generated as slots fill, and results advance winners (and losers, in double elimination) without manual bracket edits
- **Flexible entry** - Open registration, application review, or invite-only, with an optional check-in window before the start
- **Free agents** - A solo player pool that captains can draft onto their rosters
- **Hosting for anyone eligible** - Individuals, organizations, and leagues can host
- **Branding and prizes** - Banner, logo, sponsor tags, prize pool, and a prize breakdown
- **Trophies and org stats** - Completion awards top-three placements and records a champion win for organization hosts and members

## Who Can Host

Hosting is available to any account that is eligible and not restricted:

- **Any paid subscription tier** (Basic, Standard, or Premium), **or**
- **An account at least 30 days old** on the Free tier

Competitively banned accounts cannot host. The number of tournaments you can run at once scales with your tier. See [Creating a Tournament](/tournaments/create) for the full flow and the [Quick Reference](/reference) for the limit table.

<Note>
	Organizations and leagues host through their owners and admins - an org OWNER or ADMIN can host as the
	organization, and a league OWNER or ADMIN can host as the league.
</Note>

## How to Enter

1. Open a tournament's public page while registration is open.
2. Pick your team and choose the active roster (at least the tournament's required team size).
3. Depending on the tournament's entry mode:
	- **Open** - your team is entered immediately.
	- **Application** - your team applies and the host approves or denies it.
	- **Invite only** - you accept an invitation the host sent to your team.
4. If check-in is required, check in during the check-in window before the tournament starts.

Solo players without a team can join the [free-agent pool](/tournaments/free-agents) instead and wait to be drafted.
