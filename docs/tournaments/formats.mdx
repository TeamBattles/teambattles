---
title: "Formats"
description: "Single elimination, double elimination, round robin, and Swiss - and how each is scored"
---

Every tournament runs one of four formats. You choose the format on the Format step of the [creation wizard](/tournaments/create), and it cannot be changed to a different structure once the bracket is generated. Each format has a maximum entrant count:

| Format               | Maximum entrants |
| -------------------- | ---------------- |
| Single elimination   | 128              |
| Double elimination   | 128              |
| Swiss                | 64               |
| Round robin          | 32               |

Your subscription tier caps the participant count too. The effective maximum is the lower of the format ceiling and your tier's participant cap.

## Single Elimination

Every match is decisive - the loser is out and the winner advances. A field that is not a power of two is filled with byes in the first round so seeds advance cleanly. The last team standing is the champion.

## Double Elimination

A team must lose twice to be eliminated. The first loss drops a team from the winners bracket into the losers bracket, where a second loss ends its run. The winners-bracket finalist meets the losers-bracket survivor in the grand final.

Because the winners-bracket finalist reached the grand final without a loss, you can grant it an advantage:

| Grand-final advantage | Effect                                                                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **None**              | The grand final is a normal series with no advantage.                                      |
| **Win head start**    | The winners-bracket finalist starts the grand-final series ahead by a set number of wins (1-3). |
| **Map selection**     | The winners-bracket finalist is given map-selection advantage for the grand final.         |
| **Bracket reset**     | If the losers-bracket team wins the grand final, a second grand final is played to decide the champion, since the winners-bracket finalist had not yet lost. |

<Note>
	The advantaged party is always the winners-bracket finalist. With **Bracket reset**, both teams
	enter the deciding grand final even, so the true champion is whoever wins the second grand final.
</Note>

## Round Robin

Every team plays every other team. You choose whether the field plays **one pass** (each pairing once) or **two passes** (each pairing twice). Standings rank teams by wins, with head-to-head and map differential as tiebreakers.

## Swiss

Teams are paired each round against opponents with a similar record, and no team is eliminated mid-event - everyone plays the same number of rounds. If the field has an odd number of teams, one team receives a bye that round.

- **Round count** - defaults to a value based on the field size (roughly the base-2 logarithm of the entrant count) and never exceeds one less than the entrant count. You can also set the round count explicitly.
- **Byes** - awarded to the unpaired team on odd rounds and counted toward its record.
- **Buchholz** - the standard Swiss tiebreaker (the sum of a team's opponents' scores) breaks ties between teams on equal records.

## Per-Round Best-Of

Series length is chosen from **Bo1, Bo3, Bo5, and Bo7**. You set a default best-of for the whole tournament and can override it for individual rounds.

A common setup is a deeper series for the decider. For example, run every round as **Bo5** but override the **grand final to Bo7**:

- Default best-of: `Bo5`
- Grand-final override: `Bo7`

Each round snapshots its best-of when the bracket is generated, so overrides apply to the rounds you set them on.
