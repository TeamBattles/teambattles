---
title: "Creating a Tournament"
description: "Build a tournament with the five-step creation wizard"
---

Before you start, confirm you are eligible to host: you need any paid subscription tier **or** an account at least 30 days old, and you must not be competitively banned. Your subscription tier also sets how many tournaments you can run at once and the maximum participant count. See the [Quick Reference](/reference) for the limits.

Open the creation wizard from the Tournaments area. The wizard has five steps, and you can jump back to any completed step from the stepper.

## The Five Steps

<Steps>
	<Step title="Host & game">
		Choose who hosts the tournament - yourself, an organization you administer, or a league you
		administer. Then pick the game, an optional playlist, the team size (players per team), and a
		tournament name (3-80 characters). The host you pick determines the subscription tier used for
		limits.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Format">
		Select the [format](/tournaments/formats) (single elimination, double elimination, round robin,
		or Swiss) and the participant range. The maximum is capped by both the format's ceiling and your
		tier's participant cap, whichever is lower. Set the default best-of and any per-round overrides,
		the seeding method (manual, random, or registration order), and format-specific options such as
		the grand-final advantage, Swiss round count, or round-robin passes.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Registration">
		Choose how teams enter - **Open**, **Application**, or **Invite only** - and the visibility
		(Public or Unlisted). Set the registration window, whether check-in is required and how many
		minutes before the start it opens, whether the free-agent pool is enabled, and the tournament
		start time.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Match rules">
		Configure the default match setup that generated tournament matches inherit: game mode, map
		selection, platform, input device, region, and any game-specific options. A game mode is required.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Branding">
		Add optional art and presentation: banner, logo, description, rules, social links, sponsor tags
		(capped by your tier), and prize details (pool, currency, breakdown, and description).
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Save Draft or Submit for Approval

When you finish the wizard you have two choices:

- **Save draft** - creates the tournament in a draft state so you can keep editing it later from the host admin panel. Nothing is published yet.
- **Submit for approval** - creates the tournament and sends it to TeamBattles staff for review in one step.

Either way, the tournament is created first and you are taken to its host admin panel. If a submit fails after creation, the tournament still exists as a draft and the admin dashboard's approval banner lets you submit it again - no work is lost.

<Note>
	The wizard also auto-saves your in-progress form to your browser, so a partially filled wizard is
	restored if you navigate away and come back before creating the tournament.
</Note>

## What Approval Means

New tournaments are reviewed by TeamBattles staff before they can open registration. While your submission is awaiting review you cannot open registration; once approved, the approval banner clears and you can open registration from the admin panel.

<Note>
	Staff can run in a relaxed mode that only reviews publicly listed tournaments. When that mode is
	active, an **Unlisted** tournament is approved automatically on submit and skips the review queue.
	Publicly listed tournaments are always reviewed.
</Note>

Editing certain **material** fields after approval (such as name, format, participant range, schedule, or prizes) sends only those changes back for re-approval. The tournament keeps running on its last-approved values until staff accept the changes. See [Administration](/tournaments/admin#settings-and-re-approval) for the full list.
