---
title: "Free Agents"
description: "Join a tournament as a solo player, or draft free agents onto your roster"
---

The free-agent pool lets solo players enter a tournament without a full team, and lets team captains fill roster gaps by drafting from that pool. A host enables the pool when creating the tournament.

## Joining the Pool

To join a tournament's free-agent pool:

1. Open the tournament page while the pool is open for joins.
2. Add yourself to the free-agent pool, optionally with a short note about your availability or role.

<Warning>
	Some games require a linked platform account (for example, a Call of Duty tournament requires a
	linked Activision account). If the tournament's game requires a connection you have not linked, you
	cannot join the pool until you link it in **Settings > Connections**.
</Warning>

You can only be in a tournament's pool once. If you are already listed as available or already picked up, you cannot join again.

## Being Picked Up

A team captain, co-captain, or organization owner running a participating team can draft you from the pool onto their roster. When that happens:

- You are added to that team's tournament roster as a free-agent pickup.
- You receive a notification that you were picked up, linking to the tournament.

You cannot be drafted onto more than one roster in the same tournament.

## Withdrawing

You can leave the pool at any time while you are still available. If you have already been picked up, you must be removed from the team's roster first - either by the team or the host - before you can leave the pool.

## Host and Staff Removal

Tournament managers (the host, host managers, and tournament staff) can remove a free agent from the pool. If a picked-up free agent is removed from a team's roster, they return to the pool as available so another team can draft them.

<Note>
	Being in the free-agent pool is not the same as entering with a team. Teams enter through
	[registration](/tournaments); free agents wait to be drafted onto one of those teams.
</Note>
