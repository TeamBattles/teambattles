Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 supports match creation, challenges, leagues, and tournaments across 16 game modes and a 43-map roster, alongside SR rank tracking and full [Strategy Editor](https://teambattles.gg/strategy/editor) support.

## Requirements

<Warning>
	**Activision connection required.** All team members must link their Activision account.
</Warning>

Link your Activision account:

1. Go to **Settings > Connections**
2. Click **Connect** next to Activision
3. Authorize the connection

## Team Size

| Setting          | Value      |
| ---------------- | ---------- |
| Minimum          | 1 player   |
| Maximum          | 12 players |
| Default (casual) | 6 players  |
| Competitive      | 4 players  |

Pick the exact size when you create or edit the match: the wizard opens on the casual
default and lets you choose any size between the minimum and maximum. Your choice also
caps the active roster, so the lineup and the team size always agree. The Map Pool
control (Competitive or Flex) only decides which maps are available; it never changes
the team size.

The strategy editor always plans at the competitive size, so a new Black Ops 7 board
opens with 4 tokens per side.

## Playlists

**Core** uses standard health and HUD. **Hardcore** reduces health, limits the HUD, and enables friendly fire. See the [Black Ops 7 rules](https://teambattles.gg/rules/call_of_duty_black_ops_7) for the private match settings each mode requires.

## Map Selection

During match creation, choose:

- **Selection Type** - Competitive or Flex pool. Black Ops 7 opens on Competitive.
- **Preference Mode** - Veto, Preferred, or Manual. Black Ops 7 opens on Veto.

| Mode          | What you pick                                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Veto**      | One map to ban from the pool                                 |
| **Preferred** | Your favoured maps, up to half the released maps in the pool |
| **Manual**    | The exact maps to play, picked directly                      |

### How Map Selection Works

1. Both teams submit their preferences
2. The system analyses both teams' selections
3. Weighted random selection determines the final pool
4. Maps preferred by both teams are more likely
5. The final pool fairly represents both preferences

## Rank Tracking

Black Ops 7 is the only Call of Duty title on TeamBattles with a rank ladder. Ranks are **manually entered** rather than synced, across two modes (Ranked Play and Warzone Ranked), on an SR scale running Bronze through Iridescent.

See [Settings > Game Stats](/settings/game-stats) for the entry steps and the full tier table.

## Strategy Editor

42 of the 43 maps are drawable in the [Strategy Editor](https://teambattles.gg/strategy/editor), each with a minimap and derived spawn positions. Fringe ships cover art only and does not appear in the map picker.

### Overlay Layers

Black Ops 7 ships both marker toggles and zone overlays, toggled independently of the selected mode:

- **Markers** - spawn points, POI labels, and objective markers for Domination, Hardpoint, and Search and Destroy
- **Zone overlays** - Domination zones and Hardpoint zones

</Tip>
