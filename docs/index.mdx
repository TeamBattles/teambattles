## Currently Supported Games

- **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4** - 10 launch modes across 12 maps, 6v6 casual and 4v4 competitive
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops 7** - Multiple game modes with competitive and flex map pools
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops 6** - 11 game modes, 40 maps, 6v6 casual and 4v4 competitive
- **Counter-Strike 2** - 5v5 Premier and Competitive matchmaking with strategy editor support
- **Valorant** - Competitive 5v5 with full map pool
- **League of Legends** - Competitive 5v5 with all maps
- **Rainbow Six Siege** - Strategy Editor only, with 27 maps and 106 official blueprint levels;
  matchmaking is not supported

See the [Supported Games](/games) page for the full list, including every title available in the [Strategy Editor](/strategy/managing-strategies).
